# Final scientific interpretation

**Decision: CONDITIONAL GO.** A reportable claim is that, under the frozen arrangement-defined population quartets and Ag3.10 genotype-call filters, pooled nucleotide Patterson's D is strongly shifted inside the 2La interval relative to flanking/outside 2L, with the sign depending on quartet orientation. This shift is spatially broad rather than limited to a single boundary or top-contribution window. It is not an unbiased causal effect of inversion status.

H1: Supported descriptively and by physical-block uncertainty for the frozen designs: inside D is far from zero. H2: Supported for frozen inside/outside contrast; outside and separate flanks are near zero. H3: Spatial correspondence with topology statistics is strong, but the Stage 2R quartet does not exactly match the nucleotide population samples; the evidence is a descriptive co-localization, not an exact matched-quartet test. H4: No claim about branch-length distortion is established by this validation package. H5: Spatial and outgroup robustness are supported; sample composition, geography, and read-level callability robustness remain unresolved. H6: Not supported: the combined evidence does not uniquely identify MSRC over introgression, ancestral population structure, ILS, or selection-linked history.

The A versus B sign reversal is an important contradiction to any claim that there is a single arrangement-independent direction of nucleotide asymmetry. C/D show large contrasts under smaller groups, including C_i n=3, and are not independent replication. A.melas outgroup changes magnitude. The block jackknife captures broad spatial dependence at chosen physical block lengths but does not address population sampling confounding or all ascertainment mechanisms.

Interpretation should mention suppressed recombination as the genomic context, while leaving causal direction open: suppression may precede or follow rearrangement. Inversions can preserve ancestral arrangement polymorphism; geography and population structure, introgression, ILS, selection, SNP ascertainment, reference effects, and structural complexity can all contribute. Nonzero D alone does not prove introgression or confirm MSRC.
