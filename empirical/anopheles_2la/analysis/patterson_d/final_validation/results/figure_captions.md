# Figure captions

**Figure 1.** Observed 50-kb window nucleotide Patterson's D across 2L for designs A and B with A. merus outgroup. Each point is one observed source window; no smoothing or interpolation is used. Shading marks 2La (20,524,058–42,165,532 bp, AgamP4 coordinates). A and B use distinct P3 groups and opposite signs inside 2La.

**Figure 2.** Window-level nucleotide D and Stage 2R topology-frequency contrast within 2La for A and B. `D_Patterson` is the ABBA/BABA nucleotide statistic. `Delta_q=q_arrangement-q_species` is derived from Stage 2R topology frequencies (q_species, q_arrangement, q_third). These tracks do not use identical individual quartets and are descriptive; neither metric is a transform of the other.

**Figure 3.** Pooled inside and outside nucleotide D for A/B primary and melas outgroup sensitivity, plus C/D sensitivity designs. Error bars are 500-kb physical-block delete-one jackknife 95% intervals. Symbols vertically offset within each design to show the two region estimates.

**Figure 4.** Mean called chromosomes and mean passing/informative variant records per 50-kb window across flanks, inside, and outside for A/B. These are variant-site callability diagnostics, not callable-base coverage or nucleotide diversity.
