# Manuscript methods (draft)

We calculated Patterson's D from diploid biallelic Ag3.10 SNP genotype calls on the AgamP4 2L reference using the frozen 50-kb grid (988 windows). Four ordered population quartets were retained: A (A. gambiae standard, A. gambiae inverted, A. coluzzii standard, outgroup), B (A. gambiae standard, A. gambiae inverted, A. coluzzii inverted, outgroup), C (A. coluzzii standard, A. coluzzii inverted, A. gambiae standard, outgroup), and D (A. coluzzii standard, A. coluzzii inverted, A. gambiae inverted, outgroup). A. merus was primary; A. melas was a sensitivity outgroup. Sample counts and IDs followed the frozen metadata tables.

At each site, genotype allele frequencies were computed from called diploid chromosomes. Sites were excluded for multiallelic genotype codes, >25% missing genotypes across the queried samples, insufficient group calls, missing/nonbinary/polymorphic outgroup, or the requested MAC filter. A fixed diploid outgroup polarized REF/ALT; frequencies were flipped when the outgroup was ALT. Fractional contributions were ABBA=(1-p1)p2p3 and BABA=p1(1-p2)p3. Regional D was the ratio of summed contributions, `(sum ABBA - sum BABA)/(sum ABBA + sum BABA)`, not the mean of window ratios. MAC≥2 was a sensitivity filter.

The 2La structural interval was 20,524,058–42,165,532 bp inclusive. Boundary-overlap windows were excluded from inside/outside summaries while retained in tracks. Uncertainty used delete-one physical-block jackknife at 250-kb, 500-kb, and 1-Mb scales; nearby windows/sites were not treated as independent. Nucleotide D and Stage 2R gene-tree topology-frequency contrasts are different estimands. The available Stage 2R arrangement/species quartet does not exactly reproduce the nucleotide population samples, so their spatial comparison is descriptive.
