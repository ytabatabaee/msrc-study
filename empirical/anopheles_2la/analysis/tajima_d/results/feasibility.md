# Anopheles 2La Tajima's D status: VALIDATED

Completed all-site 50-kb and 100-kb tracks for 37 diploid A. gambiae/A. coluzzii samples on the full AgamP4 2L arm, including species pooled, homozygous standard/inverted, and locality groups with at least two individuals. Genotypes are joined with the validated `gamb_colu` mask; every chunk's positions were checked against non-N AgamP4 reference positions. Within-group D, S, pi-total, thetaW, per-callable-base pi/thetaW, call/multiallelic counts, regional block summaries, and existing-track joins are saved in this directory.

The species-wide background contrast is not causal: arrangements are unevenly distributed by locality, pooled species include geographic structure and heterokaryotypes, and group-specific complete-call masks differ. See `anopheles_tajima_d_report.md` for results and this limitation.
