# Patterson's D results

The eight-window pilot produced valid nucleotide-site Patterson's D values for all eight frozen designs and both filters. Descriptive pooled primary inside/outside values are summarized in `pilot_report.md`; the pilot is a feasibility experiment and does not estimate a chromosome-wide effect or uncertainty. Patterson's D and Stage 2R `D_topology` are separate estimands, and no result is interpreted as proof of introgression or MSRC causation. The 988-window scan remains unrun.
