# Fire-ant chr16 Tajima's D status: EXPLORATORY

Completed pooled (108), SB (78), and Sb (30) haploid interpretations across all 96 existing BUSCO-mapped local chr16 windows. The pooled/background contrast uses one common panel complete across all 108 males. Source GT calls must be homozygous diploid-coded male genotypes with DP>=3; sample and per-window missingness audits are included.

The local FreeBayes VCF is variant-only and the analysis is restricted to selected BUSCO gene bodies. The full segregating-site ascertainment process and invariant callability are unavailable; outputs are not unbiased population-wide classical SFS estimates. See `fire_ant_chr16_tajima_d_report.md`.
