# Inversion-level sampling limits

The annotations are the existing Stolle-axis outer envelopes recovered from the
source Rmd; they are not replaced with Yan-assembly coordinates. The published
midpoint rule is retained. In(16)1 has 47 assigned author windows (47 with
informative D sites); In(16)2 has four assigned windows; In(16)3 has zero.
Thus nucleotide support is dominated numerically by In(16)1, while the In(16)2
estimate is descriptive and based on four windows. No In(16)3-specific D is
estimable.

The In(16)3 annotation's outer endpoint is 16,478,110 bp, while the source
midpoint-classification cutoff is 16,339,881 bp. This discrepancy is retained
and disclosed, not harmonized. Outer-envelope overlap, midpoint assignment,
and exact SNP-base overlap are different quantities. The last is unavailable:
there is no continuous scaffold-to-chromosome lift for individual BUSCO genes
or SNP positions. Mapped assembled lengths (about 9.59, 0.84, and 1.20 Mb) are
component sums and the envelopes include gaps/unplaced span; they do not imply
continuous callable sequence.
