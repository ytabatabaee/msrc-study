# Figure captions

**Figure 1. Window-specific nucleotide D across chr16.** Q1 and Q2 D estimates
are shown at Stolle author-window midpoints; a separate track shows how many of
the four BUSCO genes per window have VCF records. Points are separated and no values
are interpolated. The shaded supergene and Stolle inversion envelopes
are chromosome-window annotations, not continuous SNP-base coordinates.
In(16)3 has zero source-midpoint-assigned nucleotide windows.

**Figure 2. Nucleotide D and exact induced quartet topology.** Matched windows
show D_site and D_topology=q3−q2. The tree track summarizes within-window
sampled quartet frequencies and is not an independent site dataset. Coordinate
gaps are retained.

**Figure 3. Genotype-filter sensitivity.** Points show pooled regional D over the
predeclared DP and call-fraction grid; labels report informative SNP counts.
Sparse or zero counts are low-information results and do not demonstrate
robustness.

**Figure 4. Callability and window-level influence.** Supergene window D is
plotted against variant-site call rate, with a delete-one-window pooled D
distribution by quartet. Frozen geography/outgroup subsets retain the signal;
individual leave-one-out and per-gene influence remain uncomputed. GT:DP lacks
allele depths and mapping-quality evidence, so this figure does not rule out
mapping or copy-number bias.
