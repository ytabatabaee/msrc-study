# Inversion-annotated fire-ant figure captions

Three published inversions, In(16)1–3, distinguish Sb from SB. The analysis continues to treat the frozen chr16:11,680,438–27,917,498 span as one recombination-suppressed supergene analysis region. Hatched outlines mark Stolle-mapped outer coordinate envelopes; labels give the approximate amount of assembled mapped sequence, because exact component intervals are not available in the public source deposit. Window coverage varies: Stolle's source midpoint assignments place 47 windows in In(16)1, four in In(16)2, and none in In(16)3. Our frozen tree and nucleotide tracks are therefore not separately resolved within every inversion. Inversion annotations are structural context, not direct local cM/Mb or a quantitative recombination-rate track. They do not imply that inversions alone cause all recombination suppression, nor that each inversion independently produces a significant topology switch. The published introgression interpretation and alternatives including ancestral polymorphism remain distinct hypotheses.
