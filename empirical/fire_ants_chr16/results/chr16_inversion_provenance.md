# Chromosome-16 inversion coordinate provenance

The target coordinate axis is the Stolle et al. chr16 analysis axis (`Si_gnGA` / `gng20170922wFex.fa`). Stolle Supplementary Fig. 13 says Yan et al.'s inversion breakpoint regions were mapped by aligning the closest protein-coding genes to the Stolle assembly. The exact outer envelopes below were recovered from Stolle's public [`Topology weighting/twisst_interpretation.rmd`](https://github.com/wurmlab/2021-fire-ant-social-supergene-introgression/blob/bdc7823941680fa560e61b6467af9f77950f64b0/Topology%20weighting/twisst_interpretation.rmd), in the `inversions` data frame (SHA256 `7192b5819ecef9257ef584b68379e5fac2f45cca34e64d4976876a6d5b63440b`). The supplementary PDF is [here](https://wurmlab.com/publications/2022-recurring-adaptive-introgression/rai-supplementary-Information.pdf) (retrieved PDF SHA256 `f8357d89af425c198d53f3ce8f4de8ef8ead2ad8e49cc48db4f0388d878f797c`); Stolle paper DOI: [10.1038/s41467-022-28806-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28806-7); Yan paper DOI: [10.1038/s41559-019-1081-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1081-1).

| Name | Stolle target outer extent (1-based inclusive) | Assembled mapped length in SI |
|---|---:|---:|
| In(16)1 | chr16:17,972,919–28,149,888 | about 9.59 Mb |
| In(16)2 | chr16:16,509,882–17,962,688 | about 0.84 Mb |
| In(16)3 | chr16:11,732,910–16,478,110 | about 1.2 Mb |

These coordinates are mapped **outer envelopes** across scaffold placements. Stolle's source calculates assembled lengths by summing component `chr_end-chr_start`; that placement CSV is not in the public source archive. Therefore the envelope span is not the true inverted-base length and may include unplaced/gap space. Figures use hatched outlines and show both the envelope and the reported assembled length. Internal component segments and exact base-level overlap cannot be reconstructed from the available files.

The upstream Rmd assigns window midpoints using separate strict inequalities: In(16)1 `17,972,920 < mid < 28,149,888`; In(16)2 `16,509,882 < mid < 17,962,688`; In(16)3 `11,732,910 < mid < 16,339,881`. The In(16)3 midpoint cutoff differs from its outer envelope endpoint (16,478,110); this is retained and flagged, not silently harmonized. These rules reproduce Stolle's published window labels. They are separate from our frozen `chr16_supergene` interval, chr16:11,680,438–27,917,498.

Yan's source-axis intervals in the existing audit are In(16)1 14,549,064–24,031,576 (9.483 Mb), In(16)2 13,705,210–24,030,990 (10.326 Mb), and In(16)3 12,612,565–13,683,100 (1.071 Mb). These are not overlaid because they are on the Yan SB reference axis. The names are consistent in Stolle Fig. 13 and its Rmd region definitions; the SI prose has an apparent typo calling the largest 9.59-Mb inversion In(16)3, although the figure, code, and later 1.2-Mb description identify the 9.59-Mb region as In(16)1. The mapped assembled lengths differ from Yan's full source inversion sizes, especially for In(16)2, because the Stolle assembly contains only partial mapped sequence; they are not evidence of a renumbering.

Coordinates in the source are GRanges-style one-based inclusive starts/ends. No chromosome-scale individual BUSCO gene coordinates or individual VCF SNP lifts are present in the frozen inputs. Consequently exact analyzed-gene-base coverage, SNP-base overlap, and uncovered inversion component subintervals are reported as unavailable. Window-envelope overlap is tabulated separately as a bounding proxy. Nucleotide informative counts are assigned to windows under Stolle's midpoint rule and are not exact inversion SNP counts.
