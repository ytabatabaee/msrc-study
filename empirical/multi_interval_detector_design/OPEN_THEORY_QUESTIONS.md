# Open theory questions

1. **Continuous observable law.** The finite-M operators return quartet
   topology and terminal pattern probabilities. A general analytic law for
   coalescence-event times under the full species-tree recursion has not been
   implemented or validated.
2. **Observation model.** Empirical `A(w)`, `B(w)`, SNAPP times, NJ distances,
   and ML patristic lengths are not interchangeable with true coalescence
   times. Their estimator and mutation-rate model is not part of the theorem.
3. **Time-only regimes.** It remains to determine, by predeclared simulation,
   whether the current hierarchical MSRC model yields a useful regime with
   coalescence-time changes and negligible quartet-topology changes.
4. **Microscopic dual.** The transpose/Feynman--Kac operator is exact for the
   hierarchical simulator model but has not been proved to be the ancestral
   dual of one unified microscopic Moran reproduction process.
5. **Rate heterogeneity.** Common window-wide multipliers have simple ratio
   invariances; branch-specific and lineage-specific variation does not. A
   model-based correction is still missing.
6. **Spatial dependence.** Window-level trees are correlated and grids have
   gaps. Calibrated null distributions for scan statistics remain open.
7. **Species-tree generality.** The validated full recursion covers the current
   four-taxon hierarchy and simulator inheritance rule. Larger species trees
   and general unequal population histories require further operators.
