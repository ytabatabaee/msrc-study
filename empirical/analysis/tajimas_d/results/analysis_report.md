# Empirical Tajima’s D audit and results

## Inventory and estimability

The repository-wide inventory covers Anopheles 2La, Atlantic cod LG01/02/07/12, house mouse t-complex, fire ants chr16, Neurospora L10, Neoaves chr4, Heliconius, Microbotryum, sunflowers, Primula, monkeyflowers, and songbird PIPs. Dataset-specific source, sample, ploidy, assembly, window, callability, and blocker details are in `dataset_feasibility.tsv`. Anopheles is VALIDATED for within-group callable SFS; cod, fire ants, and Neurospora are EXPLORATORY due to source ascertainment/callability limits; mouse and the population-genotype-inadequate datasets are BLOCKED. Cross-species phylogenomic groups are never pooled to manufacture a population SFS.

The shared implementation is `empirical/analysis/tajimas_d/tajimas.py`. Classical D uses the standard fixed-n estimator: pi-total is summed pairwise heterozygosity, theta_W=S/a_n, and the denominator is sqrt(e1*S+e2*S(S−1)). Fixed complete-case chromosomes are required per window; n<=3, S=0, or invalid variance produces NA with a reason. Eight unit tests pass, including hand calculations and sufficient-statistics equivalence. A prior independent `pegas::tajima.test` check matched to six decimals. No neutrality-test p-values are inferred because linkage, demography, structure and selection violate standard null assumptions.

## Dataset results

### Anopheles 2La

The Ag3.10 all-site genotype stream was scanned across AgamP4 2L (1–49,364,325) for 37 diploids (*A. gambiae* 26, *A. coluzzii* 11), using the validated `gamb_colu` mask, fixed complete group genotypes, and 50-/100-kb grids. Every queried position was checked against the non-N reference and every mask value against the official site-filter source. Callable denominators include masked, non-N invariant and biallelic sites with complete genotypes; observed multiallelic positions are excluded. Twenty-two pooled/arrangement/locality groups yielded 32,604 group-window rows across resolutions; 50-kb tracks include 988 windows/group. On the pre-defined 20,524,058–42,165,532 inversion, pooled Tajima D rises by +0.259 inside vs outside in *A. gambiae* (−1.863 vs −2.121; 500-kb block interval +0.204 to +0.319) and +0.376 in *A. coluzzii* (−1.250 vs −1.626; +0.326 to +0.431). Standard/inverted groups show little shift in *A. gambiae* inverted and *A. coluzzii* inverted (the latter n=3 diploids), while standard groups shift by +0.251 and +0.185. Pooled callable per-base pi rises from 0.01150 to 0.01717 in *A. gambiae* and 0.01151 to 0.01655 in *A. coluzzii*. The descriptive pooled-minus-unweighted-arrangement D contrast is less negative inside by +0.132 and +0.287, respectively. Species-wide arrangement contrasts span geography and are not causal within-locality comparisons. Matched tracks show positive D association with pi and dXY and opposite-sign relationships with species vs arrangement quartet support; see the dataset report and 500-kb block intervals. This adds SFS shape information, not an independent genealogy measurement.

### Fire ants chr16

The existing FreeBayes variant-only VCF was evaluated only within the 96 existing local chr16 BUSCO-mapped gene-tree windows (52 `chr16nr`, 44 `chr16r`; local Stolle/gng20170922wFex axis). Frozen primary-Argentina assignments give 78 *S. invicta* SB and 30 Sb males; homozygous diploid-encoded male GTs are interpreted as haploid and DP>=3 is required. Complete group calls fix n within each window. Classical observed-panel D is computed, but complete segregating-site discovery is unverified; no invariant/callable genome denominator or per-base diversity is claimed. For pooled/SB/Sb, inside-minus-outside mean D is −0.006 (1-Mb block interval −0.186 to +0.073), +0.111 (+0.001 to +0.228), and +0.453 (+0.194 to +0.565). On the common set of sites complete in all 108 males, the pooled-minus-unweighted-background D contrast is −0.594 inside vs −0.150 outside (difference −0.443; 1-Mb block interval −0.495 to −0.271); the weighted version changes by −0.285. Thus pooling does not elevate D above background groups in this panel. Interpretation remains exploratory because of gene ascertainment, variant-only source, and local coordinate bridge.

### Atlantic cod

Eleven named populations (two or three diploid fish each; four or six haplotypes) have separate estimates in 250-kb existing windows on the gadMor2 study LG axis; singleton `icc` is excluded. Arrangement labels are population-level, not individual haplotype assignments. Four supergene Relate VCFs contain 530,017 retained records and yield D for 1,914 population-window rows. For `avc` (two fish), inside means are LG01 −0.143 (66 windows), LG02 −0.087 (21), LG07 −0.067 (36), LG12 −0.196 (51). The outside-supergenes Relate VCF has zero records on all four target LGs, so no inside/outside contrast can be estimated. The retained Relate panels may omit segregating sites and lack invariant/callable denominators. SNAPP population-tree divergence is not substituted for gene-lineage coalescence or nucleotide SFS evidence.

### Neurospora L10

The existing filtered variable-site genotype matrix provides 18 resolved haploid isolates (mat-A 8, mat-a 10); four unresolved isolates are excluded. On the study/2509 coordinate axis and existing gene windows plus 50-/100-kb grids, LGI 1.5–8 Mb pooled D averages 3.316 inside vs 1.536 in flanks (difference +1.780; 100-kb block interval +1.417 to +2.114). mat-A and mat-a differences are −0.808 and −1.058; descriptive pooled-minus-background D contrast increases +2.714 (+2.223 to +3.162). The matrix is filtered and variable-site-only, so these are ascertainment-conditional; callable per-base pi/theta are unavailable. This established prior result was not rerun in the current follow-up.

### House mouse and other datasets

House mouse remains BLOCKED. Existing chr17 summary tracks are SNP-conditioned and do not provide individual call blocks; heterozygous carriers cannot be assigned as pure t haplotypes. The recoverable input needed is original study individual genotype/gVCF blocks, plus phasing-aware assignment of t versus standard haplotypes and a consistent call mask. No estimate is fabricated. Neoaves mostly has cross-species single representatives; songbird PIP presence genotypes are not nucleotide allele counts. Heliconius, sunflowers, Primula, and monkeyflowers lack suitable population genotype/alignment panels. Microbotryum has too few independent haplotypes per defensible population group and paired assembly sequences are not independent chromosomes. Their specific classifications are in the feasibility table; no pooled cross-species D is reported.

## MSRC relevance and limits

Under a neutral genealogy, expected unfolded SFS counts obey E[xi_i]=mu E[L_i], where L_i is total genealogy branch length subtending i descendants. However, Tajima D standardizes pi−theta_W using a random S-dependent denominator, so E[D] is not obtained by standardizing expected branch lengths, and its sign is not fixed under MSRC. The Anopheles callable tracks provide the strongest current SFS comparison and show concurrent inside increases in pi and pooled D, but geography and structure are competing explanations. Fire ants and Neurospora show opposite pooled/background contrast direction under different, ascertainment-conditioned sampling designs; this does not by itself support or reject a common mechanism. Cod lacks matched outside genotype data and mouse lacks valid haplotype calls. No cross-system parameter fit or causal MSRC claim is made. Future work should add same-population arrangement comparisons and same-panel flanking genotypes, resolve mouse phased genotype blocks, and validate variant ascertainment for the exploratory SNP panels before treating them as population SFS estimates.

Run `PYTHONPATH=empirical/analysis/tajimas_d python3 -m unittest discover -s empirical/analysis/tajimas_d/tests -v` for estimator and integration checks. Dataset-specific reproduction commands and caveats are in each dataset's `analysis/tajima_d/results/` report. Cross-dataset tables and figures are under `empirical/analysis/tajimas_d/results/` and `figures/`. No commit or push was made.
