# Empirical Tajima's D audit and results

## Scope and inventory

The repository inventory covered the population-genetic and phylogenomic datasets under `empirical/` and their local inputs under `data/`: Anopheles 2La, Atlantic cod (LG01/02/07/12), house mouse t-complex, fire-ant chr16, Neurospora L10, Neoaves chr4, Heliconius, Microbotryum, sunflowers, Primula, monkeyflowers, and songbird PIPs. Aggregation/design directories (`cross_dataset_cu`, `cross_system_boundary_detection`, `combined_quartet_simplex`, and `multi_interval_detector_design`) are not separate population-genotype datasets. Dataset-level sources, sampling units, ploidy, coordinates, and blockers are enumerated in `dataset_feasibility.tsv`.

Only Neurospora supported a complete local calculation from validated population assignments in this run. The shared implementation is `empirical/analysis/tajimas_d/tajimas.py`; it was unit-tested and checked against R `pegas::tajima.test` on a hand-built four-sequence alignment (D=0.167655795, pegas=0.1676558). Seven tests pass. Reproduce with `PYTHONPATH=empirical/analysis/tajimas_d python3 -m unittest discover -s empirical/analysis/tajimas_d/tests -v`.

## Estimator and limits

For fixed n completely called haplotypes at a site, pairwise diversity is `1 - sum_i p_i^2` (equivalently `[n^2-sum_i c_i^2]/[n(n-1)]`); window pi-total is the sum across retained sites. `theta_W=S/a1`, with `a1=sum(1/i, i=1..n-1)`. Classical `D=(pi_total-theta_W)/sqrt(e1*S+e2*S*(S-1))`, using the standard sample-size constants. n<=3, S=0, or nonpositive variance yields NA with a reason. Missing genotypes are handled by complete-case sites within a fixed sample group/window; sample n does not vary among retained sites. Multiallelic records are excluded from the Neurospora biallelic analysis and counted in the audit. SNP-panel D is conditional on ascertainment; it does not identify invariant-site callability or per-base pi/theta. No neutrality-test p-values are interpreted because linkage, structure and selection violate classical independent neutral-locus assumptions.

## Neurospora L10 results

The genotype matrix has 2,265,369 records, 26 isolate columns, no duplicate coordinates, no malformed rows, 50,123 globally multiallelic rows, and 1.95% missing calls overall. The primary L10 comparison includes 8 resolved mat-A and 10 resolved mat-a haploid isolates (18 pooled); CJ07/CJ11/CJ12/CJ18 were excluded as unresolved. The existing minimum-20-call/MAC1 matrix is variable-site-only. The analysis computed D for pooled, mat-A, mat-a, and the descriptive `Delta_D_Tajima = D_pooled - (D_mat-A+D_mat-a)/2` across the existing gene-tree windows and physical 50-kb and 100-kb grids. No invariant positions are fabricated; callable-base pi and theta are NA.

The pre-specified LGI suppressed interval is 1.5–8.0 Mb on the study/2509 coordinate axis. At 50 kb, pooled D averaged 3.316 inside (129 windows) versus 1.536 in flanks (59 windows), difference +1.780; 100-kb block-bootstrap 95% interval +1.417 to +2.114. Mat-A D averaged -0.796 inside (127) versus 0.012 outside (59), difference -0.808 (interval -1.117 to -0.502). Mat-a D averaged -0.432 inside (128) versus 0.627 outside (59), difference -1.058 (interval -1.320 to -0.807). The descriptive Delta_D averaged 3.931 inside versus 1.217 outside, difference +2.714 (interval +2.223 to +3.162). These intervals resample contiguous 100-kb blocks and are descriptive spatial uncertainty, not neutrality-test p-values. The strong pooled/background contrast is potentially MSRC-relevant, but it is conditional on the ascertainment panel and may also reflect demographic history, selection, linkage, or missingness. It adds SFS-shape information beyond the existing pi/dXY/quartet summaries, but it is not an independent replication of them. See per-window files and the LGI figure in `empirical/neurospora/analysis/tajima_d/`.

## Other datasets and interpretation

The remaining taxa are not reported as if they had estimates. Atlantic cod VCFs contain very small population samples (typically 2–3 diploid fish), population-level rather than individual arrangement labels, and no demonstrated complete segregating-site/callability record. House mouse local resources lack usable individual genotype blocks; mixed heterozygous carriers cannot be treated as t haplotypes. Fire-ant local analysis is SNP-conditioned and has mapped local chr16 genes, but this run did not complete the full genotype scan needed for a fixed-complete per-window SFS. Anopheles has validated callable-base tracks and sample/karyotype metadata; however no locality×species stratum contains both homozygous arrangements, so arrangement-specific pooled/background contrast is not identified by current sampling. For these four, no new Tajima D effect is asserted. The other phylogenomic-only datasets lack population nucleotide samples or enough independent haplotypes; cross-species pooling and topology/branch-length substitution are invalid.

### Biological conclusion

Neurospora is currently the only result in this run that directly separates pooled from independently assigned genetic backgrounds. Its pooled D increases within LGI while each background-specific D decreases, a contrast consistent with an altered pooled site-frequency spectrum under background mixture, but not uniquely predicted by MSRC. It cannot yet distinguish the MSRC mechanism from demographic structure, selection, or ascertainment. No cross-dataset mechanistic fit is justified. The next high-value analyses are a bounded Ag3.10 genotype extraction using the validated 2La callable windows, then targeted fire-ant VCF parsing; cod requires source ascertainment verification, and mouse requires source gVCF blocks plus phasing-aware haplotype reconstruction.
