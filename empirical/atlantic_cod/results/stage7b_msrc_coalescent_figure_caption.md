Figure. Theorem-linked Atlantic cod coalescence-time decomposition. Panel A shows raw population-pair inside residual MRCA times after subtracting each pair's fully outside baseline; horizontal markers show arrangement-class medians and raw points are dependent population-pair observations. Panel B shows the full Delta_coal estimate as a large black point and leave-one-population-out estimates as small gray points; the zero line is shown and LG07 is more variable. Panel C compares existing topology Delta_D with Delta_coal, with zero reference lines; LG07 lies near Delta_D=0 but above Delta_coal=0. The plot is descriptive and does not imply pair independence or a four-point correlation. Published SNAPP MCC point estimates and chromosome-aware spatial nulls were used.
