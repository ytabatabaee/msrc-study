# Methods

The primary detector fits a least-squares piecewise-constant interval model to ordered 250-kb windows. Outside the candidate interval, left and right flanks share one background mean; inside the interval has a second mean. Candidates may be internal, left-censored at the first observed window, or right-censored at the final observed window. A no-change model is always available. Minimum segment size is three observed windows.

The primary penalty is BIC-like: `p log(n)` for a censored candidate and `2 p log(n)` for an internal candidate, multiplied by a predeclared penalty factor of 1. The implementation uses genomic coordinates for interval reporting and keeps observed gaps instead of filling them. Topology uses two centered quartet-simplex contrasts; time uses `A_opposite_minus_same`; the joint model equalizes the two-dimensional topology block and one-dimensional time block before combining them. `Boundary topology D` is a prespecified one-dimensional sensitivity, and `Boundary topology forced` removes the penalty only to report the best forced topology interval for the LG07 diagnostic; neither is the primary detection decision.

All primary fits are completed before the frozen inversion coordinates are read. Accuracy uses overlap-positive windows, full-reference IoU, observed-domain IoU, and separate censored boundary errors. Sensitivity uses minimum-segment and penalty grids, joint modality weights, and deterministic leave-one-contiguous-block-out perturbations. These stability summaries are descriptive and are not confidence intervals.
