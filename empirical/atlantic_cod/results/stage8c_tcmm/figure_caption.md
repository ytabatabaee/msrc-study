# Stage 8c figure captions

**Figure A.** Calibrated Stage 8b root ages are compared with raw TCMM root
metric heights. The panels are separated because TCMM is not generally a
chronogram.

**Figure B.** Stage 8b calibrated node ages are shown in the left panel. The
right panel shows descendant-set matched TCMM metric heights at lambda 0. The
panels are separated because the raw TCMM outputs are not ultrametric and are
not automatically calibrated divergence-time trees.

**Figure C.** All-minus-outside internal-node shifts for Stage 8b and TCMM.
TCMM values are metric-tree summaries, not calibrated time estimates.

**Figure D.** Pairwise fit diagnostics across individual TCMM reference trees.
The residual statistic compares predicted metric-derived pairwise MRCA heights
with aggregated Stage 7 MRCA-time summaries and is labeled as a diagnostic,
not as TCMM's optimization objective.
