To connect the Atlantic cod analysis directly to the arrangement-conditioned coalescence result, we decomposed pairwise MRCA times relative to each population pair's collinear baseline. Because the same pair sets contribute to every window, this residualization changes the window-level arrangement contrast only by a constant and therefore leaves the original inside-versus-outside Stage-7 contrast unchanged. Its role is interpretive: it expresses the signal in the same form as E[T_D]-E[T_S] and enables population-pair and leave-one-population-out decomposition.



Across all four supergenes, opposite-arrangement population pairs showed greater coalescence depth inside the rearranged region than same-arrangement pairs. The primary circular-shift test was significant for all four linkage groups. LG01, LG02, and LG07 were also supported by the physical-coordinate placement sensitivity analysis, whereas LG12 was not significant under that secondary spatial null.



LG07 was notable because its topology contrast was essentially zero while its coalescent-time contrast remained strongly positive, providing an empirical example in which genealogy depth changes without a corresponding detectable topology shift. Its pair-level magnitude was heterogeneous, so it should not be treated as the cleanest realization of a homogeneous two-state model.



The four linkage groups are not iid replicates and population pairs are not independent observations. The leave-one-population-out ranges were LG01 0.7525--0.7624, LG02 0.6527--0.6576, LG07 0.3754--0.7129, and LG12 0.3229--0.3296.
