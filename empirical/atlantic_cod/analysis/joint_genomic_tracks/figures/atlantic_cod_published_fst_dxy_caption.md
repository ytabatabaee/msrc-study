# Figure notes: published cod F_ST and d_XY

The two figure sets plot the gadMor2 source-data tracks from Matschiner et al. (2022), Extended Data Figure 3, for LG01, LG02, LG07, and LG12. Each point is one published 250-kb sliding-window estimate at the supplied window center. Purple shading marks the frozen supergene intervals used in the local cod genealogy analysis. Points are not connected, so missing windows are not interpolated. The cod source window rows without a matching local genealogy window are still plotted because they are valid published source observations.

The F_ST panel uses the published `f_st` column (Weir-Cockerham estimator on the authors' filtered biallelic SNP data). The d_XY panel uses `d_xy*`, the sum of cross-population mismatch across retained SNP records divided by the 250-kb physical window length. Because the authors filtered to SNPs and removed sites invariant across the two population groups, this d_XY track is SNP-ascertained and is not an all-sites unbiased per-base divergence estimate. The groups are the authors' arrangement-oriented comparison groups and are not the local Patterson's D quartets. See `source_data/README.md` for provenance and estimator details.
