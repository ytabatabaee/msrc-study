**Figure 2. Binned local-tree distances across Neurospora linkage group I.** (a) Faint lines show the two independently documented matched pairs, CJ03 (mat A)/CJ04 (mat a) and CJ15 (mat A)/CJ16 (mat a); the prominent line is their equal-weight mean within each 100-kb bin. For each pair the plotted contrast is d(mat A, *N. hispaniola*) − d(mat a, *N. hispaniola*), where d is patristic distance in substitutions per site. This is not the manuscript's arrangement-informed A(w): the Neurospora design has mating-type pairs but no independent same- versus opposite-arrangement pair classes. (b) B(w) is the mean of the six unordered pairwise patristic distances among CJ03, CJ04, *N. crassa*, and *N. hispaniola*. Values are arithmetic means of available 50-variable-site window estimates in non-overlapping 100-kb bins; empty bins remain gaps. The shaded interval is the independently reported 1.5–8.0 Mb introgressed/recombination-suppressed region. All branch lengths are local BIONJ substitution distances per site, not CU or calibrated time. The two matched pairs are each documented mat-A/mat-a homokaryons.
