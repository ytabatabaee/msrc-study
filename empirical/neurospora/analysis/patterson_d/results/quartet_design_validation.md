# Quartet design validation

The target candidate is `((P1=L10 mat-a, P2=L10 mat-A), P3=N. hispaniola), O=N. discreta`. Corcoran et al. independently use *N. discreta* as the external taxon in species triplets and identify strain 8879 as a separate basal long branch in their Neurospora phylogeny. This supports the external-outgroup choice and a baseline in which L10 mating-type backgrounds are sister to one another relative to the donor-associated *N. hispaniola* lineage. Their report of excess L10 mat-A/*N. hispaniola* derived-allele sharing in the suppressed region is the alternative hypothesis, not the basis for selecting O.

The quartet was frozen before inspecting any D values: P1=L10 mat-a (CJ02, CJ04, CJ06, CJ08, CJ10, CJ14, CJ16, CJ20, CJ22, CJ24); P2=L10 mat-A (CJ01, CJ03, CJ05, CJ09, CJ13, CJ15, CJ19, CJ23); P3=*N. hispaniola* (8817@999); O=*N. discreta* (Ndis@999). The four unresolved CJ isolates remain excluded. Sensitivity designs use the independently documented matched pairs CJ03(A)/CJ04(a) and CJ15(A)/CJ16(a).

The primary population sizes are unequal (10 mat-a and 8 mat-A verified samples) because four labels remain unresolved; allele frequencies will be calculated from called verified samples per site under a predeclared 50%-of-group call threshold. The alignment has an exact reference match at all 2,265,369 genotype coordinates and mapped outgroup calls, so the quartet is valid for nucleotide analysis.

The existing gene trees do not contain *N. discreta*, so there is no exact matched four-taxon `D_topology=q3-q2` from these trees. Any comparison with published TWISST weights or the available N. crassa quartet is a separate, related-estimand spatial association.
