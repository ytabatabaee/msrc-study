# Outgroup feasibility

The target quartet is `((L10 mat-a, L10 mat-A), N. hispaniola), N. discreta`. The source ABBA–BABA study uses *N. discreta* for independent polarization. The user-provided ZIP matches the Dryad expected checksum and contains an `Ndis@999` FASTA record for each of LGI–LGVII. The 2509 reference-coordinate mapping is validated against the matrix's `2509@6` sample: exact base concordance at all 2,265,369 genotype coordinates under 1-based POS→column POS−1, with zero mismatches.

At genotype SNP coordinates, Ndis has A/C/G/T calls at 301,014/331,711 sites inside LGI 1.5–8.0 Mb, 149,665/174,635 outside the LGI interval, and 1,474,160/1,759,023 across LGII–LGVII. Gaps and ambiguous states are excluded. No reference allele is treated as ancestral.

The rooted nucleotide quartet is feasible. Exact four-taxon gene-tree comparison remains unavailable because Ndis is absent from the published local Newick trees; comparisons to TWISST weights or the N. crassa quartet must be reported as distinct, related-estimand spatial analyses.
