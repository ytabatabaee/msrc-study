# Sample-assignment provenance

The genotype matrix and Newick tips use matching CJ labels. Corcoran et al. (2016) Figure 1 explicitly labels 17 L10 strains by mating type. The Nature Communications study of rearrangements identifies CJ02 as the alternative mating-type homokaryon from the same heterokaryon as CJ01; with CJ01 explicitly labeled mat A in Corcoran et al. (2016), CJ02 is mat a. This is a biological pair assignment, not a sample-number or local-tree inference.

CJ07, CJ11, CJ12 and CJ18 remain unresolved. Martin & Van Belleghem report 10 mat-A and 12 mat-a samples and publish TWISST resolutions/weights, but the deposited `.topos` lists only the three resolutions and the weights contain no individual group membership. The totals cannot identify which of the four unresolved isolates occupy the remaining group slots. Exclude these isolates from any future sample-frequency analysis unless independently documented.

Two matched pairs are verified and usable as a small sample sensitivity design: CJ03(A)/CJ04(a), and CJ15(A)/CJ16(a). A candidate subset would set P1=(CJ04,CJ16), P2=(CJ03,CJ15), and P3=8817@999. This is a sample roster only; it cannot be rooted or polarized without a callable, validated *N. discreta* sequence.
