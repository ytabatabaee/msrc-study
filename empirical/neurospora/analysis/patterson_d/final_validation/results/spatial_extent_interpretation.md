# Spatial extent of L10 D asymmetry

The frozen 1.5–8.0 Mb LGI tract has pooled D=+0.8712. Its complement has D=+0.4460, so the original outside value is not a near-zero control. The 250-kb immediate left and right flanks are also high (+0.7191 and +0.7869). This directly contradicts an interpretation that high D is restricted to the published tract.

The flanking profile indicates a decline with distance, not a clean step at the reported boundaries. At 100 kb, the immediate left/right bands have D=+0.821/+0.852; the remaining distant left/right segments have +0.377/+0.276. At 250 kb, immediate flanks are +0.719/+0.787, while distant left/right regions are +0.127/+0.019. At 500 kb, the immediate bands remain +0.700/+0.602, while distant regions are approximately 0. At 1 Mb, immediate bands are +0.574/+0.455; the more distant segments are near zero. This supports a broad elevation extending into adjacent LGI sequence, with most of the high asymmetry concentrated in the focal tract and neighboring segments. The 1.5–8.0 Mb boundaries are published approximate biological intervals, not demonstrated D change points or exact breakpoints.

The LGI complement's +0.446 is a mixture of elevated immediate flanks and much lower distant regions. Its pooled value therefore depends on the definition of “outside.” A 250-kb distant-LGI baseline is near zero only when the high flank bands are excluded; the 500-kb distant estimates are also near zero, while 1-Mb zones still contain some elevated sequence. These comparisons are descriptive and linked; the distant portions are not established neutral controls.

The secondary whole-linkage-group estimates are LGII +0.0229, LGIII −0.0041, LGIV +0.0715, LGV −0.0049, LGVI −0.0016, and LGVII +0.0069. LGIV has a weak positive chromosome-wide background and its source q3 track also has a weak shift; it is a plausible distinct segment/lineage signal, not evidence that all LGI signal shares one cause. No formal change-point search was used. Fixed physical bins are shown only where informative-site support exists, without treating adjacent bins as independent replicates.

Possible causes of the broad LGI profile include linked introgressed ancestry extending beyond the published interval, ancestral or population structure, multiple genealogy segments, variable divergence/selection history, and SNP/outgroup ascertainment. Current data demonstrate the spatial profile, not which cause generated it.
