# Audit of the circular-shift spatial test

The original implementation forms a 100-kb LGI series of ABBA and BABA contributions, including zero-contribution bins, and circularly rotates that series while keeping the 1.5–8.0 Mb focal mask fixed. It recomputes the pooled inside-minus-outside D contrast for all 95 shifts. The reported p=0.010526 is 1/95: the observed zero shift is included among 95 total rotations, leaving 94 nonidentity rotations. The prior text describing “95 nonidentity rotations” is inaccurate.

The shift preserves the ordered 100-kb contribution vector including empty bins, but wraps the linear chromosome's right end to its left end. It also assumes that the site-contribution process is stationary around LGI and that moving the fixed focal mask over the full circular chromosome is an exchangeable null. Neither assumption is biologically established here. Physical windows are unevenly sampled in the variant panel, callability and informativeness vary, immediate flanks are strongly elevated, and the interval was independently defined from published biology rather than selected by this D scan. The test does account for the high flanks as part of the shifted contribution profile, but it does not establish a neutral local baseline or realistic relocation process.

The nominal p-value is therefore retained as a reproducible description of that circular-rotation statistic, but strong spatial-significance language is withdrawn. No substitute p-value from an arguably arbitrary shuffle is reported. The primary inferential summary is the focal/complement D contrast with physical-block uncertainty, whose outside side has few blocks (12, 6, and 4 at 250 kb, 500 kb, and 1 Mb). At 2 Mb there are too few independent outside blocks for a defensible delete-block interval. The 250-kb/500-kb/1-Mb estimates remain positive, but their intervals are descriptive and coarse-block limited.
