# Manuscript methods text (draft)

We estimated a haploid, frequency-weighted Patterson's D for the frozen quartet P1=L10 *N. tetrasperma* mat-a, P2=L10 *N. tetrasperma* mat-A, P3=*N. hispaniola* 8817@999, and O=*N. discreta* Ndis@999. P1 comprised ten verified isolates and P2 eight; four isolates with unresolved mating type were excluded. At each deposited variable site, we calculated the derived-allele frequency separately in P1 and P2 from called haploid bases, requiring at least 50% callability in each group; the P3 singleton had to be called. Sites were retained only when the L10/P3 calls contained exactly two nucleotide alleles and the unambiguous outgroup base matched one of them. Gaps, ambiguous bases, insufficient calls, multiallelic sites, and outgroup states not present in the ingroup were excluded. Fixed-ALT outgroup sites were polarized by using the other allele as derived. We summed site-frequency ABBA and BABA contributions across sites and calculated D=(ΣABBA−ΣBABA)/(ΣABBA+ΣBABA); individual site or window D values were not averaged.

The Ndis alignment uses the gap-free 2509@6 reference coordinate axis. An existing validation showed exact agreement between that reference sequence and the genotype matrix at all 2,265,369 positions. We reused this validated mapping and excluded missing/ambiguous Ndis calls sitewise. The data are a filtered variable-site matrix, not invariant/callable genomic sequence; estimates are conditional on its ascertainment.

The primary LGI interval was fixed at 1.5–8.0 Mb from published structural/recombination-suppression evidence. We report pooled D inside, in the full LGI complement, in prespecified immediate flanks of 100 kb, 250 kb, 500 kb, and 1 Mb, and in the remaining distant left/right portions. LGII–LGVII are secondary chromosome-level summaries. Uncertainty for the primary inside/outside contrast uses delete-one physical genomic blocks at 250 kb, 500 kb, and 1 Mb; the LGI outside side contains 12, 6, and 4 blocks, respectively, so those intervals are coarse and imprecise. SNPs and adjacent windows were not treated as independent replicates.

As a sample sensitivity analysis, we recalculated pooled estimates after leaving each verified isolate out and for deterministic balanced random subsets of 2, 4, 6, and 8 individuals per mating type (seed 20261118). Subsets were chosen without reference to D. A distinct analysis reports the two directly documented opposite-mating-type pairs. These are sensitivity designs rather than independent population replicates.

Published local-tree TWISST weights were compared with nucleotide D at matched 100-kb positions. The q3 topology is the unrooted split pairing L10 mat-A with *N. hispaniola* in trees containing *N. crassa*; the trees do not contain *N. discreta*. We therefore report correlations with TWISST q3 and do not interpret them as exact rooted quartet `D_topology`.
