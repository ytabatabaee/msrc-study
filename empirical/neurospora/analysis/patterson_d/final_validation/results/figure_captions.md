# Final validation figure captions

**Figure 1. LGI nucleotide D profile and information support.** Points show pooled frequency-weighted D in 100-kb physical bins with at least 50 informative sites; point size/alpha reflects informative-site count. The published 1.5–8.0 Mb interval and 250-kb/500-kb/1-Mb neighboring bands are shaded. A lower track displays informative-site counts per bin. Points are not independent replicates or confidence intervals. D is conditional on the deposited variable-site panel.

**Figure 2. Pooled D across focal, flanking, distant, and chromosome regions.** Region estimates pool ABBA/BABA contributions before D calculation. For LGI focal/complement, whiskers show existing physical-block jackknife intervals at 250 kb, 500 kb, and 1 Mb in separate points; for flanks and distant segments, 250-kb block delete-one intervals are shown only when at least three physical blocks are available. Single/few-block regions are shown without a confidence interval. LGII–LGVII values are whole-linkage-group secondary controls. The comparison does not designate LGI complement as neutral.

**Figure 3. Nucleotide D and published TWISST topology weight.** The matched 100-kb nucleotide D track is shown with TWISST q3. q3 is the unrooted split grouping L10 mat-A with *N. hispaniola* in a tree that includes *N. crassa*. Published local trees do not contain *N. discreta*; this is not an exact rooted four-taxon `D_topology` comparison. Tracks share selected variable-site inputs and physical linkage.

**Figure 4. Ascertainment and sample sensitivity.** Panel A compares the physical spans of 50-variable-SNP source windows inside and outside the LGI focal interval. Panel B shows per-window Ndis callability. Panel C shows LGI inside/outside D from deterministic balanced subsets of increasing sample size, with all draws shown. Panel D shows LGIV D for the full group and balanced draws, highlighting its sensitivity to isolate choice. The matched-pair subset is separately labeled and is not treated as a population replicate.
