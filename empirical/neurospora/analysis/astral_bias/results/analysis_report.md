# Neurospora L10 ASTRAL region-bias analysis

## Scope and data audit

This analysis uses the deposited local genealogies in `data/neurospora/raw/neurospora/trees/`, not newly downloaded sequencing data. The archive checksum and extracted tree/coordinate checksums are recorded in `results/input_manifest.json`. The 45,303 coordinate rows align one-to-one with the tree archive: 32,472 valid 26-tip Newicks and 12,831 missing-tree rows. All valid trees were included; no branch-length or topology filter was added. Tree branch lengths are BIONJ lengths from PhyML GTR analyses of 50-variable-site windows, nominal substitutions/site. Coordinates are on the study's 2509-reference axis. The focal interval is LGI midpoint 1,500,000–8,000,000 bp inclusive, matching the existing Stage 1 definition. It contains 5,767 valid trees; 26,705 trees remain after exclusion. LGII–VII contains 24,352; LGI outside the interval contains 2,353.

Mating-type assignments were taken from the prior source audit, not inferred from tree topology or sample numbering. Eighteen of 22 CJ isolates have resolved backgrounds. CJ07, CJ11, CJ12 and CJ18 remain unresolved and are excluded from background-level mapping, while all CJ isolates are mapped to *N. tetrasperma* for the species-level analysis. Two independently labeled matched A/a pairs (CJ03/CJ04 and CJ15/CJ16) were analyzed as separate sensitivity designs.

## Methods

ASTRAL-IV v1.25.4.8 was run in multi-individual mode using `-a`, `-R`, `-u 2`, `--length CULength`, `--genelength 50`, and `--sulengthtype 1`; exact commands, logs and trees are saved beside each result. CULength is the ASTRAL quartet coalescent-unit estimate. SU/site is ASTRAL-IV's integrated CASTLES-II branch-length estimate using the supplied gene-tree branch lengths and the 50-variable-site gene length. SU-mode reruns were made for all and region-excluded primary sets; their branch estimates exactly equal the SU annotations in CU-mode outputs (maximum absolute difference 0). Terminal CU/SU values are not estimated by this method and are not interpreted.

Trees are compared as unrooted bipartitions. RF distance is the symmetric difference of informative splits; branch lengths are compared only for identical bipartitions. Delta is region-excluded minus all windows. Quartet frequencies and local posterior probabilities are taken from ASTRAL annotations. Spatial uncertainty was assessed with 20 independent 250-kb block bootstrap replicates per primary design. Within each replicate, the all-window and region-excluded datasets were independently resampled within linkage groups; delta intervals therefore represent an unpaired block-bootstrap contrast. Matched-removal controls used 20 random sets of outside-region blocks per design, each removing approximately the focal 5,767 trees. These replicate counts provide coarse exploratory intervals and empirical tail areas, not fine-grained p-value calibration.

## Results

**Topology.** In the four-species analysis, the sole informative split, *N. crassa* + *N. hispaniola*, is unchanged: RF distance 0. In the six-lineage background summary, all three informative bipartitions are unchanged between all windows and region-excluded windows: RF distance 0. Both pair-specific sensitivity analyses also preserve all three splits (RF 0). Thus the focal region does not change the ASTRAL topology in these designs.

**Branch lengths.** For the species split (*N. crassa* + *N. hispaniola*), CU is 0.186027 with all windows and 0.214338 after focal-region exclusion (delta +0.028311); SU/site is 0.0933622 vs 0.108005 (delta +0.0146428). In the lineage design, the L10 A/a bipartition has CU 0.275212 vs 0.445701 (delta +0.170489), while SU/site is 0.0561585 vs 0.0314763 (delta −0.0246822). The two deeper lineage branches change by +0.045685 CU/+0.017171 SU and +0.026521 CU/+0.0139573 SU, respectively.

The matched-pair analyses reproduce substantial CU sensitivity on the L10 A/a branch (CJ03/CJ04: +0.233453 CU; CJ15/CJ16: +0.159413 CU). SU sensitivity varies in sign across those two sampling choices (−0.0070359 and +0.0197374 SU/site), underscoring uncertainty from sparse sampling of the backgrounds. Full per-branch quartet frequencies, localPP, and support annotations are in `results/branch_comparison.tsv`; all five regional subsets (all, region-excluded, region-inside, LGII–VII, LGI outside) are tabulated in `results/regional_astral_estimates.tsv`. Exact trees and subset counts are in the design folders and `results/input_manifest.json`.

**Block uncertainty and matched removals.** All 20 block-bootstrap replicates per condition recovered the corresponding point-estimate topology in both primary designs (RF 0 in every bootstrap replicate). For the L10 A/a branch, the region-excluded minus all-window CU shift was +0.170489; the 20-replicate bootstrap median was +0.178398, with a percentile range of +0.111846 to +0.232072. The SU/site shift was −0.0246822 (bootstrap median −0.0200116; percentile range −0.0442162 to −0.00993689). Both intervals exclude zero.

Matched-removal controls removed a mean of 5,883.7 trees (range 5,780–6,032) in the species design and 5,875.45 (range 5,776–6,004) in the lineage design, versus 5,767 focal trees. For the L10 A/a branch, outside-block removals produced CU changes from −0.048258 to −0.012616 and SU changes from −0.009075 to −0.002446. The observed shifts were larger in absolute value than all 20 controls for both units (plus-one empirical two-sided p = 1/21 = 0.0476, the coarsest possible resolution at n=20). The same pattern held for the other shared primary branches and for the species split. All matched-removal replicates preserved the reference topology (RF 0). These controls support the conclusion that focal-region exclusion produces unusually large branch-length shifts relative to random outside-region removals of similar size, while the small control count limits the tail-area precision. Bootstrap and control summaries are in `results/block_resampling_summary.tsv` and `.json`; the comparison figure is `figures/matched_removal_controls.png` (also PDF/SVG), and replicate-level ASTRAL output and logs are retained in `logs/block_resampling/`.

**Existing quartet signal.** The primary tree topology is unchanged despite clear branch-estimate shifts. The lineage L10 A/a branch's q1 frequency increases from 0.493741 to 0.573104 when the region is excluded, while q2 decreases from 0.330787 to 0.221672 and q3 increases from 0.175472 to 0.205223 under ASTRAL's branch-local quartet annotation. These values should be read as local support for that mapped branch, not as the prior whole-genome 4-taxon q1/q2/q3 track.

## Interpretation and limitations

These results establish that a large, genealogically atypical genomic interval materially affects ASTRAL branch-length estimates while leaving the inferred topology unchanged. The clearest effect is the L10 background branch in CU units; SU effects are less stable across paired-isolate sampling and are dependent on CASTLES-II's treatment of gene-tree lengths and variable-site ascertainment. Because each input tree is estimated from a fixed 50 variable sites, these SU estimates are model-based and should not be treated as unbiased absolute divergence times. ASTRAL's quartet-based CU estimate is likewise conditional on the gene trees and their sampling.

The focal block is physically linked and constitutes a large fraction of the dataset, so windows are not independent replicates. The block bootstrap and matched-removal controls show that the focal interval has a larger branch-length effect than the tested random outside-region deletions, but only one block width (250 kb) and 20 control replicates were evaluated. Boundary and block-width sensitivity remain outstanding. This analysis documents estimator sensitivity to including the interval, not causality from recombination suppression. Introgression history, linked selection, population structure, or other genomic history may contribute.

## Reproducibility and next steps

Run input preparation and the primary analysis with `python3 empirical/neurospora/analysis/astral_bias/scripts/run_astral_bias.py`; prepared exact tree subsets, mappings, logs, ASTRAL output trees, manifests and checksums are retained in this directory. Run `python3 -m unittest discover -s empirical/neurospora/analysis/astral_bias/tests`. Next priority is sensitivity to block widths and focal boundaries, followed by incorporating the result into the broader theory-to-data comparison. The 20-replicate block results are exploratory because percentile tails and the matched-removal tail-area are coarse; the reported plus-one empirical p-value is at the minimum resolution possible for 20 controls.
