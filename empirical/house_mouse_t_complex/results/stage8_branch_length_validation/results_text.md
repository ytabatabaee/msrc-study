# Results

The focal terminal branch is present in the frozen source trees and drives the observed distance discontinuity. Across the all-sample track, mean `B(w)` is 0.00912753 upstream and 0.322949 downstream; corresponding leave-one-out means are 0.00916046 and 0.00785359. Direct recalculation agrees with Stage 4 to maximum absolute differences of 0 for B, 3.33e-16 for A_distance, and 4.44e-16 for A_relative.

The three-filter archive comparison shows a reproducible filtering-specific pattern: the extreme focal branch occurs in the coverage-plus-allele-ratio-filtered ML trees, whereas corresponding PASS-SNP and coverage-only trees have ordinary terminal branches. This establishes that the anomaly is linked to the filtered tree product, but the exact contribution of filtering, alignment composition, ascertainment, and ML optimization remains unresolved because per-window alignments and complete inference settings are unavailable.
