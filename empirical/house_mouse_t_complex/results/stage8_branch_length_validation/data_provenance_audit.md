# Data provenance audit

The primary trees are the 4,046 metadata-matched local Newick trees in `data/house_mouse_t_complex/processed/house_mouse_t_complex_ml_5kb.tre`. Stage 0 reports 55 tips in every tree, valid Newick syntax, branch lengths in all trees, no negative branches, and exact coordinate mapping. The tree archive originates from the Kelemen & Vicoso data archive (`IST-2017-78-v1+1_Data.zip`, SHA256 `f925084a87ac3837661ad99f931da608c129ea3e590c3470030b3ba649d47877`). The archive README documents ML, NJ, and MP trees under three SNP-filtering procedures: PASS SNPs, coverage plus allele-ratio filtered raw SNPs, and coverage-filtered raw SNPs.

The committed tree file is a processed copy of the coverage-plus-allele-ratio-filtered published ML trees. Representative comparisons across the three nested published ML archives show that the extreme focal branch is present in the coverage-plus-allele-ratio product but is ordinary or absent in the PASS-SNP and coverage-only products. This verifies a filtering-product-specific reproducible anomaly, but does not identify whether the cause is allele-ratio filtering, a resulting sparse/ambiguous alignment, or downstream ML optimization. No per-window FASTA alignments or complete IQ-TREE command/version records were recoverable, so the exact mechanism cannot be independently rerun here.

The `.variant` suffix is retained as a source tip label. The frozen tip mapping identifies `Mmd_FRA4_18B.variant` as a standard noncarrier *Mus musculus domesticus* sequence; the suffix alone was not interpreted as a special branch-length encoding.
