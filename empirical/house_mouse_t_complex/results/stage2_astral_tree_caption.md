# ASTRAL tree captions

Unrooted four-group ASTRAL summaries of the published 5-kb ML gene trees,
shown as cladograms. Standard-only and t-haplotype-only recover `Q_SPECIES`,
whereas the mixed standard + t treatment recovers `Q_T_ALT`. *M. spretus* is
used only to orient the four-group display. Focal edge annotations report
split-specific CU, localPP, and biologically named quartet frequencies.
`q_species`, `q_t_alt`, and `q_other` are named biological topologies; they are
not synonymous with ASTRAL q1/q2/q3 across different inferred focal splits.
ASTRAL inference itself is unrooted.

The population figure shows the original seven-population standard-only and
mixed trees alongside a six-population t-haplotype-only tree with SPRE removed.
RF = 2 refers only to the matched seven-population standard-versus-mixed
comparison; the six-taxon t-only tree is not assigned an RF distance against
the seven-taxon trees. A common-six three-treatment analysis is also retained
in `stage2_common6_population_astral.tsv` for matched taxon-set comparisons.
