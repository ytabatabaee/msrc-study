# Methods

ALL_TIPS support fractions were read from the frozen Stage 2 fixed quartet scan and filtered only by treatment. The presentation-standardized topology contrast is D(w)=q_t_alt(w)-q_species(w), the negative of the historical delta_species_alt column. TTT is retained as a supplementary status-pattern track from the frozen arrangement-pattern spatial table and receives the same sign transformation.

A_distance and A_relative were copied exactly from the frozen Stage 4 ML-tree distance table. Their units are substitution distances; they are not calibrated divergence times or coalescence times. Native plots retain the original coordinates and split line segments across unusually large coordinate gaps. Matched 250-kb tables use arithmetic window means within bins anchored at 5 Mb.
