**Figure. Unified house-mouse t-complex topology and divergence-distance tracks.** (a) ALL_TIPS D(w) = q_T - q_S, where positive values favor the designated t-associated alternative topology. (b) A_distance is the frozen Stage 4 t-versus-standard patristic-distance contrast in ML substitution units. (c) A_relative divides that contrast by the mean non-M. spretus pairwise distance. (d) The ALL_TIPS support fractions for the species-history, t-associated alternative, and third quartet resolutions. Shading marks the four frozen t-complex inversion intervals; dashed lines mark their endpoints. The 5-kb panels preserve observed genomic gaps. The 250-kb figure uses arithmetic means within fixed coordinate bins for both topology fractions and distance summaries. The supplementary figure compares the ALL_TIPS and TTT topology sampling schemes; it does not imply that the schemes should agree.
