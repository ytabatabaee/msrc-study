# Figure caption

**Figure 1. Corrected chr17 nucleotide and exact gene-tree quartet tracks.** Four published t-complex inversion intervals are shown on the common mm10/GRCm38 chromosome-17 axis. Rows show the three frozen exploratory designs. The nucleotide panel plots corrected fixed-REF plus fixed-ALT population-level D in 100-kb bins; points are absent where no denominator exists. The exact matched topology panel plots equal-locus D_topology=q3−q2 only over chr17:5.0–37.055 Mb, the local-tree coverage. The informative-site panel reports nucleotide informative-site counts. Blank regions are unsupported, not interpolated. Full chromosome-17 estimates and matched-span estimates have different genomic coverage. The genotype and tree panels also represent different biological estimands because VCF calls are unphased diploids and carrier tree tips are reconstructed pseudo-t sequences.
