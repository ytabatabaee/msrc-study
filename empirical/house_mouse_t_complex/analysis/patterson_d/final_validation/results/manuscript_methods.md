# Methods text

We analyzed the Harr et al. AllMouse PASS VCF on mouse chromosome 17, mm10/GRCm38, by indexed regional queries, using the pre-frozen 55 sample IDs and three P1/P2/P3/O population designs in `config/frozen_quartet_members.tsv` (D1 4/12/13/8; D2 8/13/12/8; D3 3/7/13/8). Only biallelic single-nucleotide A/C/G/T records were retained. Diploid `0/0`, `0/1`, and `1/1` GTs contributed ALT dosages 0, 1, and 2; population ALT frequency was the mean across completely called individuals, with incomplete calls omitted. At least one complete call per ingroup population was required. Outgroups were retained only when all eight *M. spretus* samples were complete and fixed for REF or ALT. Fixed-REF sites used ALT frequencies; fixed-ALT sites used derived-allele frequencies `1−ALT`. Polymorphic or missing outgroups were excluded.

For derived frequencies p1, p2, p3, per-site contributions were ABBA=`(1−p1)p2p3` and BABA=`p1(1−p2)p3`; contributions were pooled before calculating D=`(ABBA−BABA)/(ABBA+BABA)`. A zero denominator is undefined. VCF positions are one-based; assignment used `(POS−1)//window_size` to zero-based half-open windows. D was aggregated at 5, 50, 100, 250, and 500 kb. Tree window labels from Kelemen & Vicoso were restored by the documented +5 Mb offset; only exact overlapping intervals were compared. Published inversion coordinates were taken unchanged from the independent GRCm38/mm10 interval table.

The exact sample-ID tree comparison classified each induced four-tip combination once by four-point distances into q1=(P1,P2)|(P3,O), q2=(P1,P3)|(P2,O), q3=(P2,P3)|(P1,O); ties were unresolved. Within-tree frequencies were calculated among resolved quartets, each local tree was weighted equally, and D_topology=q3−q2. Quartet combinations are not independent loci. Pearson/Spearman spatial correlations and 500-kb block resampling are descriptive; intervals with sparse coverage and circular-shift p-values under irregular support are not treated as robust general spatial inference.

The VCF genotypes are unphased diploid calls. Local-tree carrier tips are reconstructed pseudo-t-haplotype sequences, not verified homolog-specific copies of the VCF calls. These estimands therefore do not constitute t-haplotype-specific nucleotide D. Population structure, geography, subspecies history, admixture, ancestral structure, incomplete lineage sorting, SNP ascertainment, and linked variation remain possible explanations.
