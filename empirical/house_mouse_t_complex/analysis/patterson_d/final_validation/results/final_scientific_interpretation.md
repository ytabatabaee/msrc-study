# Final scientific interpretation

The fixed-REF plus fixed-ALT corrected population-level estimates are D1 +0.19955, D2 +0.13186, and D3 +0.08359 over the full chr17 scan. Original fixed-REF-only results (D1 −0.13038, D2 +0.02569, D3 +0.05725) are superseded and retained only as historical comparisons. Corrected inversion union versus full-contig complement is +0.39396 vs +0.04378 (D1), +0.24055 vs +0.02010 (D2), and +0.26206 vs +0.01446 (D3). These are separate from the exact tree matched span (5.0–37.055 Mb): inside/outside D_site is 0.353/0.463, 0.197/0.347, and 0.242/0.216 for D1–D3, respectively. The D1/D2 direction reversal reflects a change in comparison coverage and denominator: full-contig complement D pools all outside-inversion nucleotide windows, while the exact-tree complement contains only 222 matched 5-kb loci in the local collinear gaps/flanks; the distal part of In(17)4 is also beyond tree coverage. This is a different estimand, not a demonstrated computation error. The high contribution density and strongly heterogeneous interval-specific values make simple “inversion effect” language inappropriate.

Exact sample-ID gene-tree estimates are D_topology=q3−q2: +0.0785 (D1), −0.1613 (D2), +0.0705 (D3). Whole matched-span nucleotide D_site/topology correlations are positive at 50–500 kb (see `spatial_validation.tsv`; at 100 kb r=+0.688/+0.629/+0.711). These are descriptive spatial co-variation measures. Correlations remain positive after centering within source interval classes: at 100 kb, the all-region residual r is +0.582/+0.602/+0.660 and inversion-only residual r is +0.596/+0.607/+0.667 for D1–D3. Collinear/flank residual r is +0.501/+0.629/+0.645, but uses only 15 windows across four 500-kb blocks and is descriptive. Independent 250/500/1,000-kb delete-block jackknife and existing block bootstrap intervals are in `spatial_validation.tsv`; the matched-span D_site contrast intervals include zero. The circular-shift implementation drops unsupported blocks and rotates the remaining ordered block summaries, so it does not preserve physical gaps or the irregular inversion/window structure; its p-values are descriptive, not a robust general spatial null. D relative to zero and inside-minus-outside D are separate questions.

The estimator and exact quartet calculations pass the documented synthetic checks. Population relationships and sampling structure mean the three frozen designs are exploratory, particularly D3's three carriers; broad subspecies topology makes each frozen P1/P2 pair a plausible sister grouping, but does not remove carrier-selection or population-structure confounding. The indexed transversion/callability scan completed (65/65 chunks); exact tree-matched signs and interval contrasts were stable at all three call-rate thresholds. The individual-level scan did not complete: the remote endpoint truncated BGZF at file offset 48,567,409,620, and no leave-one-out or balanced-subset outputs are reported. No sample-level robustness claim is warranted. Most critically, unphased VCF genotypes and reconstructed pseudo-t tree tips represent different biological observations. No result is direct evidence about phased t-chromosome alleles or uniquely establishes introgression/MSRC.
