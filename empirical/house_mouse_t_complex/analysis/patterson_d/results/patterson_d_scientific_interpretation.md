# Scientific interpretation

1. **Can D be computed?** Yes, from the verified indexed public nucleotide VCF, for SNP site patterns only.
2. **Valid quartets?** Three rooted exploratory population contrasts are frozen in config. *M. spretus* is a close, separate outgroup supported by the study. The designs do not represent conventional species-tree nulls; they compare arrangement-defined subsets with standard population groups.
3. **Stronger inside inversions?** No consistent strengthening by absolute D: the combined inversion |D| differences are design-dependent (reported with block-jackknife SE in the main report and TSV). They cannot be interpreted as t-haplotype effects without phasing and stronger population matching.
4. **Interval variation?** Individual intervals are summarized separately in `patterson_d_jackknife.tsv`.
5. **Same regions as gene trees?** Exact 5-kb window matching is tabulated for the Mmd quartet. For ((P1,P2),P3),O, ABBA corresponds to derived sharing by P2+P3 and BABA by P1+P3; the available q_t_alt/q_species full-tip tracks do not use those exact sample populations, so any co-location is descriptive, not a direct topology match.
6. **Robustness?** Resolution pooling is evaluated. Transversions, outgroup alternatives, and alternate SNP filters remain untested; see robustness table.
7. **Recombination partitioning?** These scan summaries do not establish a recombination-partitioned signal. Spatial compatibility with locally persistent genealogies is only a hypothesis; no formal location concordance test was done.
8. **Alternatives:** population structure, admixture or introgression, ancestral population structure/polymorphism, incomplete lineage sorting, SNP ascertainment, and polarization/missingness.
9. **Neoaves/Anopheles:** qualitative analogy only: all use nucleotide D and spatial aggregation, but taxon sampling, SNP/alignment source, and quartet interpretation differ. Numerical comparisons are inappropriate.

No nonzero D uniquely supports introgression or MSRC.
