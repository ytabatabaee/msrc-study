# Phase and population-structure limits

The AllMouse VCF contains unphased diploid GTs. In a t-carrier, the two alleles at a SNP can include one t-bearing and one standard homolog, but the genotype alone does not say which is which or connect phase across SNPs. This analysis therefore estimates population-level allele-frequency asymmetry, not t-chromosome-specific ABBA/BABA. Ancestral-state correction does not solve phase.

Kelemen & Vicoso constructed pseudo-t-haplotype sequence profiles for the 15 carriers by retaining SNPs inferred to be t-specific after subtracting heterozygous SNPs shared with noncarriers; the archive's carrier tree tips are those reconstructed pseudo-t sequence tips. They are useful individual-matched tree observations, but they are not independent fully phased calls from the same chromosome copy underlying the diploid VCF genotype. A standard-homolog allele can dilute t-specific frequency differences; low coverage, allele-frequency filtering, residual standard variants, and recombination/gene conversion can distort the relationship in either direction. The paper itself notes that residual standard variants may remain on pseudo-t profiles ([Kelemen & Vicoso 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5753869/)).

A newer published t-haplotype resource includes sequence from homozygous `t^w5/t^w5` mouse embryonic stem cells, but this is one laboratory haplotype and not phased chromosome data for the 15 wild carrier individuals in the frozen designs ([Swanepoel et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11838278/)). It is not substituted into these population contrasts. No same-individual chromosome-specific re-calling was performed.

The frozen contrasts also span subspecies and geographic origins: D1 compares French/German *M. m. domesticus* carriers and standards with standard *M. m. musculus*; D2 compares Afghanistan/Czechia/Kazakhstan *M. m. musculus* carriers and standards with standard *M. m. domesticus*; D3 compares Indian *M. m. castaneus* carriers and standards with *M. m. musculus* standards. Within-subspecies P1/P2 matching reduces some structure, but P3 and the carriers' local ancestry can still make allele-frequency asymmetry reflect geographic/subspecies history. Introgression, ancestral population structure, incomplete lineage sorting, and recombination/gene conversion remain alternatives. The correlations do not establish a single causal history.

For the MSRC paper, report the corrected estimator and the exact q3−q2 comparison as descriptive concordance, explicitly state that the sign/direction depends on quartet design and inversion interval, and avoid describing the result as phased t-haplotype evidence or as proof of introgression/MSRC. Full-chromosome nucleotide scans and the 5–37.055 Mb matched gene-tree subset must remain distinct.
