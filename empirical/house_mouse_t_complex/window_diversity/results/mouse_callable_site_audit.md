# House-mouse chr17 callable-site audit

Date: 2026-11-20. Scope: the full-chromosome tracks and inversion inside/outside bars under `empirical/house_mouse_t_complex/window_diversity/`.

## Exact inputs behind the current figure

- `scripts/analyze_full_chr17_snp_panel.py` reads the indexed Harr et al. `AllMouse.vcf_90_recalibrated_snps_raw_indels_reheader_PopSorted.PASS.vcf.gz` from the public wild-mouse VCF directory. It processes biallelic A/C/G/T PASS records, GT only. The file is variant-only: a site absent from it is not represented and must not be presumed invariant or callable. The local `.tbi` is an index, not a callable mask.
- The comparison rows come from `analysis/patterson_d/config/frozen_quartet_members.tsv`, restricted to roles P1 and P2: Mmd 4 t / 12 standard, Mmm 8 / 13, and Mmc 3 / 7. These are 47 distinct individuals across the three separately analyzed designs. P3 and O rows in that file are out of the diversity comparison. Heterozygous carriers were sitewise reconstructed from the allele unique relative to same-subspecies P2 standard chromosomes; this does not phase the t chromosome across sites.
- Windows and tracks use mm10/GRCm38 chr17, 0-based half-open intervals, [0, 94,987,271), in 5-kb windows, aligned to existing local-tree windows (which cover only 5.0–37.055 Mb). Inversion intervals come from the repository's validated GRCm38 table.
- The calculation does not read FASTA, BAM/CRAM, gVCF/reference blocks, or a callable mask. A search of the mouse data, analysis, and diversity directories found no local FASTA, alignment, gVCF, or callable-mask file. A BAM index exists at repository root but its BAM is not local.

The earlier estimates divided SNP-site sums by retained observed PASS SNP counts. This was not a per-base estimator. The full-chromosome track has been regenerated with an explicit “EXPLORATORY” title and a note that dXY and π are per observed PASS SNP, not per callable nucleotide; the inside/outside bar plot and all previous numerical contrasts remain SNP-conditioned.

## Public invariant-call evidence and attempted recovery

The same wild-mouse archive has a public indexed individual `vcf/GVCF/` directory containing reference blocks and variant records. The frozen P1/P2 IDs map to public individual gVCF/index pairs (the repository root contains matching index caches). A successful indexed query of B2C at chr17:5,000,000–5,100,000 returned mm10/GRCm38 chr17 length 94,987,271 and reference-block records with diploid GT and DP, GQ, and MIN_DP. It therefore demonstrates that invariant-call evidence exists and that missing callable denominators are not a biological impossibility. A corresponding query of several other individuals also returned the same contig length and fields.

However, a low-concurrency pilot over that same 100-kb interval across the frozen sample list was not robust: the remote `CR46.all.vcf.gz` stream failed while reading a BGZF block at compressed offset 8,170,095,228 (`truncated file`), followed by a network close timeout. Earlier higher-concurrency multi-sample queries also returned truncated-file / reconnect errors. Consequently, no complete, reconciled chr17 common-callability mask was produced. This is a transport/retrieval failure on large indexed remote streams, not evidence that all missing positions are invariant. No all-chromosome denominator or recalculated per-callable-nucleotide values are reported.

The required full gVCFs are large genome-wide files (the published directory's selected-sample archive was inventoried at roughly 363 GB compressed across 47 individuals); range reads do not remove the need to traverse substantial blocks for chromosome-wide coverage. Lower concurrency, chunk-level retries/checksums, and local staging of all chr17 gVCF slices are needed before calculating the common mask. The source directory is [publicly indexed here](https://wwwuser.gwdguser.de/~evolbio/evolgen/wildmouse/vcf/GVCF/). Kelemen & Vicoso describe their use of the wild-mouse BAM/VCF resources and heterozygous t carriers in the [original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5753869/).

## What can and cannot be concluded now

No valid numerical comparison to per-callable-base estimates can be made until those indexed slices are recovered and reconciled. In particular, absent PASS-VCF positions cannot be added to a denominator, and the observed-SNP means cannot be converted by multiplying by SNP density: ascertainment, missingness, gVCF variant records, and carrier phase ambiguity vary across the chromosome. The published results remain exploratory per-observed-SNP summaries. Their direction (higher inversion-region Hudson FST and dXY in all three designs, lower reconstructed t π in all three, mixed standard π) is unchanged because no replacement estimator was computed; its magnitude and precision may change when invariant and callable positions are handled correctly.

Further requirements before a callable-base analysis: verify the exact sample name in every gVCF header; require diploid, fully called genotypes with a declared DP/GQ rule (using MIN_DP for reference blocks); reconcile gVCF variant calls to the PASS-panel sites and conservatively mask variant or indel spans that cannot be represented in the biallelic analysis; require a consistent callable denominator for each population pair (and report any carrier-specific phase masking); then sum pairwise differences/diversity over those callable nucleotides and estimate Hudson FST as a ratio of sums over supported callable SNPs. Validate against the mm10/GRCm38 reference checksum and all-sites calls before replacing the exploratory tracks.
