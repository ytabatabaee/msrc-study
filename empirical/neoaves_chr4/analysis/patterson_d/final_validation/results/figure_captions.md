# Figure captions

## Figure A — Nucleotide D tracks

Patterson’s D in 250-kb Stiller source-coordinate windows for Columbea, N61 and N62. Gray bars show informative ABBA/BABA site counts, and gray shaded bands show the three published interval memberships under the frozen GalGal4 window-start predicate. Low-information bins are shown as gaps; site-count bars remain visible. The axis is the Stiller source coordinate; GalGal6 structural tracks and breakpoint claims are intentionally omitted because no base-level coordinate lift was applied.

## Figure B — Matched nucleotide and genealogy statistics

Matched 1-Mb bins of pooled D_site and exact four-species D_topology=q3−q2. Both statistics use only loci with all four exact species present in the archived local tree and saved nucleotide counts. q1=(P1,P2)|(P3,O), q2=(P1,P3)|(P2,O), and q3=(P2,P3)|(P1,O). Bins are linked along chr4; lines are descriptive and are not independent observations.

## Figure C — Inside/outside estimates

Pooled D_inside and D_outside for the three primary frozen designs, and ΔD=D_inside−D_outside. Bars show ±1.96 times the 1-Mb genomic block-jackknife SE. The zero-line comparison for D_outside is distinct from the zero-line comparison for ΔD. Columbea and N62 pooled ΔD estimates remain near zero.

## Figure D — Outgroup and substitution-class sensitivity

Inside-minus-outside D for all sites, transitions and transversions under the primary *Gallus gallus* and alternative *Meleagris gallopavo* outgroups. Bars show ±1.96 times the 1-Mb physical-block jackknife SE. Designs share taxa and loci and are correlated sensitivity analyses; they are not independent replicates. Transition-only counts are paired differences of stored all-site and transversion counts; transversion-only windows are less informative.
