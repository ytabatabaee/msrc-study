# Manuscript methods (validated wording)

We reanalyzed the saved locus-level ABBA and BABA counts from the original Stiller et al. intergenic nucleotide alignments for the three frozen four-taxon designs and their predeclared *Meleagris gallopavo* outgroup sensitivities. Site patterns were oriented as ABBA=A/B/B/A and BABA=B/A/B/A, with A assigned to the selected outgroup state. The estimator was D=(ABBA−BABA)/(ABBA+BABA), pooling counts before taking the ratio. The stored pipeline excludes non-ACGT symbols, IUPAC ambiguity and gaps; only biallelic patterns matching ABBA or BABA contribute. Transversion-only results were retained as a separate filter. Transition-only ABBA/BABA counts were derived per locus by subtracting transversion counts from the paired all-site counts; this exactly partitions the stored informative patterns without a new alignment scan.

The focal intervals use the published inclusive GalGal4 chr4 10-kb window-start rule: 25,030,000–32,670,000; 33,510,000–34,470,000; and 44,130,000–56,810,000. Locus membership is applied on the stored Stiller source coordinate. The intervals are not treated as exact structural breakpoints and are not overlaid directly on GalGal6 tracks.

Regional values pool ABBA/BABA counts across loci. Uncertainty and inside-minus-outside uncertainty use physical genomic delete-block jackknifing at 500-kb, 1-Mb and 2-Mb blocks; 1-Mb is the main display. Reported descriptive intervals are estimate ±1.96 block-jackknife SE. This tests `D_outside=0` separately from the regional contrast `D_inside−D_outside=0`; no site-level binomial test is used. Boundary sensitivities move all interval edges by ±250 kb or ±1 Mb on the same source-coordinate axis.

For genealogy comparison, each archived Stiller local tree was reduced to the exact four taxa for a frozen D design when all four tips were present. The three unrooted resolutions were q1=(P1,P2)|(P3,O), q2=(P1,P3)|(P2,O), and q3=(P2,P3)|(P1,O). We defined D_topology=q3−q2 to match the direction of the ABBA−BABA numerator. Nucleotide and tree statistics were pooled into matched 1-Mb bins using only loci with both a saved nucleotide count and an exact four-tip tree. Pearson correlations and 2-Mb physical-block bootstrap intervals are descriptive, account for genomic clustering, and are not used as p-values. Branch lengths are ML substitution distances.

An independent scalar reference implementation was checked against the production site counter on synthetic pattern cases. Saved pooled outputs were also recomputed from locus-level ABBA/BABA rows. The 3.6-GB alignment archive was not rescanned in this finalization; the independent scalar tests therefore do not constitute an independent raw-alignment re-decode.
