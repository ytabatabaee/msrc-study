**Figure | Chromosome-4 quartet signal and empirical mixture thresholds.** (A) Rolling per-locus QQS support for N61 and N62 along GalGal4 chromosome 4. Orange bands mark the published PNAS outlier intervals and purple bands mark the frozen primary structural mask. (B) Linear mixtures of the recovered published full-source QQS endpoints, with affected-region weight ε. Dashed lines mark the observed valid-locus weights and dotted lines mark valid q1=q2 crossovers. (C) Completed 48-taxon ASTRAL treatments shown as categorical experiments, with RF distance from T0. T0 and structural-primary share one topology, whereas PNAS removal, 500-kb blocks, and non-chr4 share the alternative topology. The QQS curves are full-source published statistics and are not exact 48-taxon quartet estimates or a fitted MSRC model.
