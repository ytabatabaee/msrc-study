Our full-data 363-taxon ASTRAL tree reproduced the cached published PNAS full-data topology exactly under canonical unrooted RF comparison. The published cached outlier-removed tree retained the same N62 relationship, with Columbimorphae intact, whereas our reconstructed outlier-removed run changed one internal split (RF 2) and placed Columbiformes with Otidimorphae. Because our gene-tree inputs were reconstructed from the named collection rather than the original aLRT-collapsed archive, this is a source-reproduction discrepancy rather than evidence that the PNAS taxon-sampling result is contradicted. The result motivates an exact-input controlled comparison; it does not by itself establish an MSRC-specific causal mechanism.
