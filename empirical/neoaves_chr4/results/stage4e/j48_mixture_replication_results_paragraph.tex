We evaluated sensitivity of 48-taxon ASTRAL-IV inference to chromosome-4 genealogy reweighting using five independent with-replacement mixtures of 62,945 gene-tree records at each of five weights (epsilon = 0.02, 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, and 0.15). All 25 runs completed successfully and produced identical CU and SU topologies with 48 taxa. The complete J48_T0 topology was recovered in 4/5 and 4/5 replicates at epsilon = 0.10 and 0.15, respectively, and first appeared at epsilon = 0.075; recovery became the majority at epsilon = 0.075. Lower weights included non-chr4 and intermediate or distinct topologies, demonstrating sensitivity in the coarse transition interval. Because each mixture is an independent resample and epsilon is a genealogy-pool sampling weight, these results establish empirical sensitivity to genomic reweighting without estimating a deterministic threshold or identifying recombination suppression as its cause.
