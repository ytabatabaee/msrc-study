# Results

The full 363-taxon T0 baseline is validated in `t0_full363_validation.md` and
standardized in `t0_reference_branch_lengths.tsv`. T0 has identical CU and SU
topology under canonical unrooted split matching; future comparisons must match
internal branches by those split identifiers and compare terminal SU branches
by exact species label. T_PNAS and the remaining treatment and random-control
comparisons are pending returned and validated Bridges-2 outputs.
