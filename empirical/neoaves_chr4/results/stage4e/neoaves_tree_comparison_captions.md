# Figure captions and Results text

## Main CU figure

**Figure X. Neoaves species-tree topology and coalescent-unit branch lengths across taxon sampling and genomic treatments.** Rectangular phylograms show the inferred ASTRAL-IV trees for a common 12-species display subset, after inference from the complete 48-taxon (top row) or 363-taxon (bottom row) datasets. Columns show all loci, PNAS outlier removal, and primary structural-region removal. Trees were pruned only for visualization. Green and magenta edges mark the competing focal splits induced by the observed T0/PNAS topology changes. Internal labels on highlighted edges are the source CU estimates; terminal CU lengths are unavailable in ASTRAL-IV and are shown as dashed schematic edges. The full 48-taxon comparison has three changed splits between the T0 and PNAS treatments (RF = 6), whereas the full 363-taxon comparison has one changed split (RF = 2). Structural-primary removal retains the T0 topology at both scales.

## Supplementary SU figure

**Figure Sx. Substitution-unit branch lengths for the same inferred trees.** Panels use the same treatment order, taxon subset, and focal split colors as Figure X. Horizontal lengths are source SU estimates after display pruning; terminal and composite-path provenance is recorded in `neoaves_display_tree_edge_mapping.tsv`. SU lengths are substitution units and are not interpreted as divergence times.

## Branch-length comparison figure

**Figure Sy. Homologous internal branch-length comparisons.** Each point compares a full-tree internal split shared by T0 and a treatment, separately for 48 and 363 taxa and for CU and SU. The dotted line is equality. Split-absent branches are excluded from length differences and are treated as topology changes. The underlying table reports shared split counts and per-split signed differences.

## Results paragraph

The completed ASTRAL-IV analyses showed the same treatment grouping at both taxon scales: all-locus and primary structural-region treatments retained the T0 topology, whereas PNAS-outlier removal, 500-kb block sampling, and chromosome-4 exclusion shared an alternative topology. The change was larger in unrooted RF distance for the 48-taxon analyses (RF = 6) than for the 363-taxon analyses (RF = 2). On the common display subset, the altered 48-taxon relationships resolve the Columbimorphae-associated grouping against a grouping that includes the sampled Otidimorphae representatives; the 363-taxon change is represented by the full-tree Columbimorphae split versus the Columbiformes + Otidimorphae split. Shared homologous branches showed median absolute CU differences of 0.00050–0.00200 in the 48-taxon comparisons and 0.00191–0.00732 in the 363-taxon comparisons, depending on treatment. These are post-inference comparisons of reconstructed inputs; the full-data 363-taxon tree matches the cached published PNAS reference, while the reconstructed PNAS-removal tree differs from the cached published outlier-removed reference by RF = 2.
