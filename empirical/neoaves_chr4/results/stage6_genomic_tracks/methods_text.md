# Methods

QQS support and coordinates were reused from the frozen Neoaves chromosome-4 locus table. q1/q2/q3 were not recomputed from inferred topology labels. For each local named ML tree, patristic distances were recalculated after requiring the same 48 exact mapped taxa in the primary distance track. Means were taken across all fixed-set pairs. Structural regions were copied from the canonical GalGal6-coordinate table. No arrangement labels were inferred from local topology, and no arrangement-informed A(w) was estimated.
