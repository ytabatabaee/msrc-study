# Neoaves chromosome-4 genomic rearrangement tracks report

## Dataset support

- Coordinate reference: GalGal6 chr4.
- QQS support rows: 18109 across three source-defined focal designs.
- Unique coordinate-mapped distance loci: 6140; complete fixed mapped-taxon observations: 2707.
- Fixed mapped set: 47 exact Jarvis-to-Stiller taxa; one source mapping is non-exact and excluded from the fixed set.
- Branch lengths and B(w) are ML substitution-unit quantities, not calibrated coalescence times.

## Supported panels

The primary topology display keeps Columbea, N61, and N62 separate. In each design q1 is the source-defined species-history resolution; q2 and q3 are separate alternatives. A single qH is not assigned across designs.

The fixed mapped-taxon arrangement-blind distance has mean 0.209755, median 0.208351, and range 0.0242136–0.38563.

## Unsupported panels

The repository does not contain an independent binary arrangement partition that would support a common opposite-versus-same A_distance or A_relative contrast. Those panels are omitted rather than manufactured. No validated coordinate-resolved recombination-rate track is available either.

## Structural annotations

Canonical structural regions used in the top panel: CR_01, CR_02, CR_03, CR_04, CR_05, CR_06, CR_07, CR_08, CR_09, CR_10, CR_11. Their boundaries are published/inferred region boundaries on the GalGal6 axis and are not treated as causal nucleotide-resolution breakpoints.

## Interpretation

The figure is comparable to the house-mouse layout in showing spatial quartet support and an arrangement-blind distance summary. It is not a direct analogue of an arrangement-informed A(w) analysis because the avian sampling lacks the required independent binary arrangement labels. Spatial concordance with structural regions is descriptive and does not establish that rearrangement caused the topology or branch-length pattern.
