# Neoaves chromosome 4 Stage 8 report

## Scientific scope

Stage 8 asks whether independently supplied structural states explain local ML-tree patristic distances beyond each species pair's chromosome-4 background distance. It does not estimate recombination rates or coalescence times.

## Findings

The structural-state sample remains six species; no additional labels were imputed. The region-specific multistate synteny table supports exploratory comparisons for CR_01, CR_10, CR_11. Pair-specific broad and strict outside baselines, residual contrasts, relative residuals, and leave-one-species-out sensitivity are provided.

The adjusted statistic is related in purpose to the arrangement-associated contrasts in other datasets, but it is not directly analogous in inferential strength: Neoaves has deep species-level comparisons, multistate states, and limited same-state replication. The topology tracks D2 and D3 remain source-defined and are compared descriptively to the one common six-species divergence analysis.

## Interpretation

Any large adjusted signal must be checked against pair composition, leave-one-species-out results, long branches, missing loci, and the broad-versus-strict baseline. A residual contrast that persists numerically does not resolve the lack of independent structural-state replication.
