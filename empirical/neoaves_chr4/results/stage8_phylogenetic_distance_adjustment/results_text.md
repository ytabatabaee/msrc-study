# Results

The independent structural-state sample could not be expanded beyond six exact local-tree taxa without unsupported state imputation. The recomputed Stage 7 eligibility set contains CR_01, CR_10, CR_11 eligible exploratory regions. Adjusted contrasts are reported for CR_01, CR_10, and CR_11 where the same-versus-different state comparison is numerically estimable. Because CR_01 has only one same-state pair and CR_10/CR_11 pairs share the same six species, these are exploratory species-level residual contrasts rather than independently replicated evidence for arrangement-associated divergence.

The three QQS designs remain separate in spatial concordance summaries. A common six-species divergence track is not relabeled as three focal-design-specific A(w) tracks.
