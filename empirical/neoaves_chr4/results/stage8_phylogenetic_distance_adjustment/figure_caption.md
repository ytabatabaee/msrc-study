# Figure captions

**Neoaves chromosome 4 Stage 8.** The topology overview retains the three
source-defined QQS designs (Columbea, N61, and N62), showing their three
resolved supports and the two contrasts (D_2=q_2-q_1) and (D_3=q_3-q_1).
Gray bands mark the independently defined canonical structural regions. The
adjusted-divergence figure shows one common six-species analysis for CR_01,
CR_10, and CR_11; it is not repeated as if it were independently estimated
for each QQS design. Broad residuals use pair-specific medians from windows
outside the focal region, while strict residuals use windows outside all
canonical structural regions. Dashed curves show equal-weight state-pair
categories. The pair-diagnostic figure displays the contributing pair-level
residuals, making the limited and shared species replication visible.

All distances are patristic distances from local ML trees in substitution
units. They are not calibrated divergence times, realized coalescence times,
or recombination-rate estimates. The adjusted arrangement contrast remains
exploratory because structural labels are multistate species-level synteny
classes and the six species provide limited independent same-state
replication.
