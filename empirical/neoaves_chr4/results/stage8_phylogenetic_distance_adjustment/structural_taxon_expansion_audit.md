# Structural taxon expansion audit

The frozen canonical state table contains 6 species labels across 11 regions, but the Stage 7 exact compatibility audit supports six exact local-tree mappings: Stork=Ciconia_maguari, Flamingo=Phoenicopterus_ruber, Dove=Columba_livia, Sandgrouse=Pterocles_gutturalis, Turaco=Tauraco_erythrolophus, Cuckoo=Cuculus_canorus.

No additional species were added. The local trees contain many tips, but assigning a state to an additional tip would require an independently supported structural classification and an exact label mapping. Relatedness, local topology, or a partial name match was not used as an assignment rule. Ciconia is retained because its exact local tip is present even though it is absent from the 47-taxon Jarvis mapping.

The canonical labels are region-specific multistate synteny/anchor states, with missing and uncertain assignments. They are independent of the local ML trees used for distances and QQS. The six-taxon limitation means all adjusted contrasts remain exploratory and cannot be treated as independent population-level replication.
