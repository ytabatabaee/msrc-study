# Neoaves Stage 11 report

This stage establishes a local-first Phlag benchmark scaffold and runs the existing locus-level detector on the original Columbea statistics. The official Phlag contract requires ordered local Newick gene trees and a labeled reference species tree; the repository's three source-defined QQS tracks are not a universal substitute. A matched four-taxon pilot is prepared from the original local trees. Results are method-agreement evidence unless the pilot is independently evaluated against an external target.

No claim that the MSRC detector outperforms Phlag is made from the overlapping structural annotations.
