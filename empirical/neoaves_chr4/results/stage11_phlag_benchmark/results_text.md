# Results

The local audit supports a chromosome-4 matched-taxon pilot rather than a full published-result reproduction. The QQS summaries are retained as a compatibility audit and are not mislabeled as Phlag inputs. The unchanged Phlag v0.2.0 default run was finite and informative for 5,619 fixed four-taxon local trees, with 1,200 state-1 windows and a measured runtime of approximately 48.94 seconds. The topology-only and topology-plus-B locus-level scans both selected 44.13--56.82 Mb under the fixed scan configuration. Detector and pilot metrics are in the TSV outputs.
