# Validation report

Stage 6 locus-level support and Stage 9 B values were read without modification. The Phlag pilot used fixed representatives and exact four-tip local-tree taxon sets for 5,619 of 6,096 Columbea loci; 477 loci lacked at least one representative and were excluded before inference. The official Phlag output had finite posterior values and a nonconstant state path. Structural-region metrics are post-fit exploratory summaries, not independent ground truth.
