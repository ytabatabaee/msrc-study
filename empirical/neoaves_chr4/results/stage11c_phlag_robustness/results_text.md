# Results

All 10 planned representative sets were prepared and all valid runs are recorded in `run_manifest.tsv`. Replicate zero was reused from Stage 11B. Phlag mean/median IoU were 0.792109/0.792416; matched-detector mean/median IoU were 0.638987/0.638987. Phlag mean/median F1 were 0.883993/0.884188; matched-detector mean/median F1 were 0.779734/0.779734. These are paired representative-selection replicates, not independent biological datasets.

The eleven evaluation regions are synteny-derived structural candidates, not experimentally verified inversion breakpoints. Region-level call fractions and denominators are in `region_stability.tsv`; window-level denominators and anomaly frequencies are in `anomaly_frequency.tsv`.
