# Matched-taxon Phlag results

Anopheles and fire-ant matched-taxon pilots were executable but numerically degenerate: their posterior anomaly probabilities were nonfinite. The house-mouse pilot was valid and informative, with finite posterior probabilities and a nonconstant state path. Its one-replicate, one-edge default state-call score was IoU 0.1326 and F1 0.2342. These values are exploratory and do not establish method performance across systems.
