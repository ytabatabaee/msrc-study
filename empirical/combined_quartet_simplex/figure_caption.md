# Figure caption

**Empirical quartet-support simplexes inside and outside annotated rearranged or recombination-suppressed regions.** Each point is one frozen genomic window or binned window-level QSS aggregate from the source analysis, with topology order harmonized as q1 = baseline/species-tree topology, q2 = arrangement-associated or focal alternative topology, and q3 = third topology. The top, lower-left, and lower-right vertices correspond to q1 = 1, q2 = 1, and q3 = 1, respectively. Blue points mark observations inside an annotated region and orange points mark observations outside; boundary observations are retained and shown in gray where present. Identical coordinates are represented by a translucent halo with area scaled by overlap multiplicity and a smaller opaque core at the unchanged coordinate; the legend illustrates n = 1, 5, 20, and 100. Circle and square cores preserve visibility when inside and outside observations coincide. The dashed line is q2 = q3, the standard MSC symmetry line for the two discordant resolutions when q1 is the species-tree topology. Atlantic cod is shown separately for LG01, LG02, LG07, and LG12. Neoaves uses the N61 100-kb bins from the existing multiresolution track, fire ants uses the chr16 Stage 4A BUSCO windows, Anopheles uses the existing 50-kb three-quartet support windows, and house mouse uses 100-kb means of the ALL_TIPS fixed-quartet scan. Counts are observation counts, not counts of individual gene-tree quartets. The figure is descriptive and does not by itself establish a causal rearrangement mechanism.
