# Mai30 MSRC comparison summaries

This report implements the two primary comparisons requested for the Mai30
pilot:

1. MSRC gene-tree discordance relative to the fixed true Mai species tree.
2. The MSRC gene-tree distribution relative to the original Mai gene-tree
   distribution for the same species-tree replicate.

The calculations are generated by
`analysis/scripts/summarize_msrc_discordance.py`. The machine-readable outputs
are `mai30_msrc_vs_species.tsv` and
`mai30_msrc_vs_original_distribution.tsv`.

For MSRC versus the species tree, the tables give mean and median normalized
unrooted RF distance, exact species-tree topology frequency, mean species-tree
split concordance, mean sampled quartet concordance, and ASTRAL RF and exact
recovery when ASTRAL output exists. The normalized RF denominator is
`2(n-3)` for a fully bifurcating unrooted `n`-taxon tree.

For MSRC versus the original Mai gene trees, independent trees are compared as
distributions and are never paired by locus. The tables give the difference in
mean RF from the species tree, empirical RF Wasserstein and KS distances,
difference in exact species-tree frequency, difference in species-tree split
concordance, mean quartet-frequency L1 distance, and mean quartet-margin
difference. The quartet comparison uses the same deterministic 1,000 sampled
taxon quartets for all trees in a replicate.

All 12 primary collections are complete: the three suppression treatments for
replicates 042, 007, 005, and 074. Each has 500 trees from 25 independent
frequency histories and 20 loci per history. The rows are marked
`analysis_scope=complete_500` and are included in the pilot summaries below.

## MSRC versus original Mai distributions

`RF delta` is MSRC mean RF minus original Mai mean RF. `RF W1` and `quartet
L1` are distribution distances, where zero indicates identical empirical
distributions for that summary.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

The realized distributional differences are small in aggregate RF space, while
quartet-frequency differences are larger for the extreme-ILS replicate 007.
This supports treating gene-tree distribution shift and species-tree inference
error as separate quantities. It does not establish a population-level quartet
reversal or ASTRAL inconsistency.

The suppression-effect analysis in `analysis/suppression_effect/` provides the
within-MSRC κ=1 contrasts, split-level replicate-007 audit, and a small
history-level ASTRAL bootstrap. The ordinary MSC remains an external control;
κ=1 is not an ordinary MSC limit because finite background dynamics remain.
