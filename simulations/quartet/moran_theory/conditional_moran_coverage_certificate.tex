\paragraph{Conditional Moran local coverage.}
Fix the finite-$M$ Moran MSRC recursion, including its binomial daughter-count
inheritance kernels, and condition on the terminal arrangement pattern
$Z=0101$. For the two-duration family obtained by varying the root duration
$T_A$ and taxon-1 terminal duration $T_1$, the joint probability recursion can
be differentiated analytically using
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}e^{At}v=Ae^{At}v
\]
and the quotient rule for
$q_i=J_i/(J_1+J_2+J_3)$. At $M=10$, $K=5$, $\rho=0.1$, and
$(T_A,T_1)=(1,0.2)$, the determinant of the $(q_1,q_2)$ Jacobian is
\[
 -1.297436435\times10^{-4}
\]
for the balanced rooted tree and
\[
 -2.619600004\times10^{-4}
\]
for the pectinate rooted tree. Centered finite differences agree with the
analytic derivatives to better than $3\times10^{-10}$ over the tested step
range. Subject to a formal certification of the displayed nonzero
determinants, the inverse function theorem gives a nonempty open subset of the
conditional quartet simplex for each rooted shape. This is a local result only;
the calculations do not prove global simplex coverage, density, or an
unconditional result.
