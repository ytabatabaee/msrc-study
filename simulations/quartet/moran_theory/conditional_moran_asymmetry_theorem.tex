\paragraph{Asymmetry control remains incomplete.}
For $Z=0101$, parameterize the conditional quartet law by
\[
q=((1-d)r,d,(1-d)(1-r)),
\qquad d=q_2,
\qquad r=q_1/(q_1+q_3).
\]
In the exact finite-$M$ Moran recursion, the audited symmetric zero-lower-
branch subfamily has $r=1/2$. In the pectinate recursion, asymmetric lower
branch durations produce valid parameter points with $r<1/2$ and with
$r>1/2$, while $P(Z=0101)>0$ in both cases. Thus no universal inequality
$q_1\ge q_3$ holds for this pattern.

These witnesses do not prove sector coverage. A global theorem would require,
for every feasible $d$, a proof that the level set $q_2=d$ contains the full
required interval of $r$ values. Neither a local Jacobian calculation nor
opposite-side witnesses supplies that level-set result. Consequently full
conditional simplex coverage and the corresponding introgression consequence
remain open.
