\paragraph{Conditional local image.}
Let $f_z(T_A,T_1)$ be the exact conditional quartet map obtained from the
finite-$M$ Moran recursion with all other parameters fixed. If the determinant
of its two-by-two Jacobian at an interior point is nonzero, the inverse
function theorem implies that the conditional image contains an open subset of
the quartet simplex.

For the parameterization audited here, the exact matrix recursion gives stable
numerical determinant ranges centered at approximately
$-1.2974\times10^{-4}$ for the balanced tree and
$-6.4949\times10^{-4}$ for the pectinate tree at $Z=0101$.
These values are numerical certificates pending a formal interval-arithmetic
bound. They do not establish global surjectivity or density.
