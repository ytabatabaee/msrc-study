# Conditional finite-\( M \) Moran quartet image: strongest established result

## Scope

This document concerns the original finite-\( M \) Moran-integrated MSRC
model implemented in `full_species_tree.py` and `unbalanced_species_tree.py`.
The species tree is fixed, the four arrangement bits are observed, and the
reported law is

\[
q_i(z;\theta)=\frac{J(z,i;\theta)}{\sum_{j=1}^3J(z,j;\theta)}.
\]

No two-regime model, arbitrary inheritance kernel, external mixture, or
introgression model is used below.

## The proved theorem: vertex closure

Let the rooted species tree be either `((1,2),(3,4))` or `(((1,2),3),4)`.
Take equal positive chromosome counts on all branches, a point-mass initial
Moran count `K` with `0<K<M`, neutral Moran dynamics, zero background
switching, and zero terminal, sister, and (for the pectinate tree)
intermediate branch durations. Let `T` be the root ancestral duration. For
the paired patterns

\[
0011\longleftrightarrow Q_1=12|34,\qquad
0101\longleftrightarrow Q_2=13|24,\qquad
0110\longleftrightarrow Q_3=14|23,
\]

the exact conditional law has the form

\[
q^{(j)}(T)=(1-S_j(T))e_j+S_j(T)r_j(T),
\]

where `S_j(T)` is the probability that the two compatible pairs have not
resolved the quartet before the root, `e_j` is the corresponding simplex
vertex, and `r_j(T)` is a probability vector. In particular, the exact
finite-parameter conditional pattern probability is positive and

\[
\lim_{T\to\infty}q^{(j)}(T)=e_j.
\]

Consequently, for both rooted shapes,

\[
\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}\subseteq
\overline{\bigcup_{z,\theta:P_\theta(Z=z)>0}
\{P_\theta(Q\mid Z=z)\}}.
\]

### Proof sketch

At zero switching and zero lower-branch duration, the two taxa forming the
paired pattern share a background at the first ancestral branch where they
can coalesce. The compatible pair has a positive coalescence rate for every
interior count. The probability that it survives to the root is therefore a
survival probability `S_j(T)` tending to zero as `T` grows. If it coalesces,
the unrooted quartet is fixed to `Q_j`; if it survives, the remaining root
process contributes a probability vector. The joint recursion preserves the
positive mass of the observed pattern, so conditional normalization is valid
for every finite `T`. The same argument applies to the pectinate tree because
the lower branches have zero duration and the first compatible pair still
determines the unrooted quartet.

The certificate generated by `analyze_conditional_moran_full_simplex.py`
checks the exact solver at `M=4`, `K=2`, `T=0,1,8` for both shapes and all
three patterns.

## What is not proved

Vertex closure is weaker than interior coverage or density of the entire
simplex. The model is not known to be closed under convex mixtures: choosing
three different parameter settings and averaging their output laws defines an
external mixture, not one Moran MSRC parameterization. A nonzero numerical
Jacobian at a point gives evidence for a local two-dimensional image, but the
repository does not yet contain a validated interval enclosure for the matrix
exponentials and hence does not present that as a formal inverse-function
theorem.

The following claims remain unresolved for the original conditional Moran
model:

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| One paired pattern covers its dominance sector | Open |
| All six strict quartet orderings are attainable | Open; only three appeared in the bounded diagnostic |
| Every interior point is attained at finite parameters | Open |
| The closure of the union is the full simplex | Open |
| A positive-length introgression overlap follows rigorously | Open |

The exact missing global lemma is either a constructive inverse/sector
coverage result or a valid internal Moran representation of arbitrary convex
weights. Neither follows from vertex limits, local rank, or numerical target
fits. Conversely, the current bounded searches do not prove an excluded
region or a universal inequality.

## Consequence for interpretation

The established result shows that, conditional on observed arrangement
patterns, the finite-M Moran MSRC model can approach each of the three quartet
vertices while keeping the rooted species tree fixed. It does not establish
full simplex coverage, unconditional coverage, or general introgression
indistinguishability. Any manuscript statement must preserve these
distinctions.
