# Neurospora sample metadata

The archive's `cra_his_L10.topos` and TWISST weights explicitly encode the four analysis groups. The local trees carry strain labels, not per-tip mating-type annotations. The published analysis reports 10 L10 mat-A and 12 mat-a samples. Do not infer individual CJ mating types from identifier parity; matched pairs CJ03/CJ04 and CJ15/CJ16 are documented in Corcoran et al. 2016. The three additional tree tips beyond the quartet are recorded in `sample_groups.tsv` and are not part of the deposited four-lineage TWISST weights.
